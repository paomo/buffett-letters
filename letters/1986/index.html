Appendix

Purchase-Price Accounting Adjustments and the "Cash Flow" Fallacy

“购买价格会计调整与‘现金流’谬误”——巴菲特最著名的会计批判文章。1986年信的正篇讨论通胀与债券，而这篇附录专门回答一个反直觉问题：同一家公司（Scott Fetzer），收购前后的GAAP利润为何差出$11.6M？哪个数字才反映真实经济价值？巴菲特用O/N双栏对照，引导股东穿透会计表象去看现金流。“所有者收益”（owner earnings）概念正是在这里首次系统提出，日后成为估值教科书的标准工具。1986年Berkshire账面价值+26.1%，但巴菲特特意用整篇附录提醒股东：账面数字本身并不可靠。

First a short quiz: below are abbreviated 1986 statements of earnings for two companies. Which business is the more valuable?

以小测验开场：两家公司利润表几乎相同，哪家更值钱？答案是“两家其实是一家”——O列是“旧”Scott Fetzer（未收购口径），N列是“新”Scott Fetzer（Berkshire合并口径）。这种教学式开头是巴菲特致股东信的一贯风格：先抛出一个反直觉的问题，再带读者一步步推导出结论，让读者自己“发现”答案比直接告知更有说服力。

As you've probably guessed, Companies O and N are the same business - Scott Fetzer. In the "O" (for "old") column we have shown what the company's 1986 GAAP earnings would have been if we had not purchased it; in the "N" (for "new") column we have shown Scott Fetzer's GAAP earnings as actually reported by Berkshire.

谜底揭晓：O与N是同一家公司——Scott Fetzer。O列是“假设未被收购”的GAAP口径，N列是Berkshire报表中的实际口径，两者只差在购买价格会计调整（purchase-price adjustments）。1986年1月6日，Berkshire以$315M收购Scott Fetzer——这是巴菲特当时最大的一笔收购，金额约相当于伯克希尔当年账面价值的三成。旗下World Book百科全书与Kirby吸尘器都是拥有品牌和直销网络的现金牛，这笔收购的会计整合成了整篇附录的素材。

It should be emphasized that the two columns depict identical economics - i.e., the same sales, wages, taxes, etc. And both "companies" generate the same amount of cash for owners. Only the accounting is different.

两栏经济实质完全相同——同样的销售额、工资、税收，同样多的现金归股东，只有会计不同。这是“实质重于形式”的浓缩表述：会计是地图，生意才是地形。巴菲特反复强调，聪明的投资者要盯住现金流产生能力，而不是会计口径下的某个数字。这一思想与他1977年提出的“用ROE而非EPS衡量业绩”一脉相承：度量标准选错，结论必然跟着错。

So, fellow philosophers, which column presents truth? Upon which set of numbers should managers and investors focus?

“哲学家们，哪一栏呈现了真相？管理者与投资者该盯住哪组数字？”巴菲特把问题上升到哲学层面，答案却是颠覆性的：两栏都是“真实”的会计数字，但没有一栏单独呈现经济真相。任何单一会计口径都可能误导——这正是他坚持在报告中同时提供多种口径（报告利润、穿透收益、所有者收益）的原因。

Before we tackle those questions, let's look at what produces the disparity between O and N. We will simplify our discussion in some respects, but the simplification should not produce any inaccuracies in analysis or conclusions.

预告方法论：简化讨论，但不牺牲准确性。下面巴菲特将逐步拆解$142.6M溢价在资产负债表上的分配过程——这是理解并购会计的必经路径。对普通股东而言，这些“会计杂技”看似枯燥，却是读懂一切收购案的钥匙：溢价去了哪里、未来哪些年度会被摊销、真实现金是否受损。

The contrast between O and N comes about because we paid an amount for Scott Fetzer that was different from its stated net worth. Under GAAP, such differences - such premiums or discounts - must be accounted for by "purchase-price adjustments." In Scott Fetzer's case, we paid $315 million for net assets that were carried on its books at $172.4 million. So we paid a premium of $142.6 million.

溢价来源：支付$315M，账面净资产仅$172.4M，溢价$142.6M。按GAAP，溢价必须通过“购买价格调整”分配到具体资产与商誉上。注意溢价比账面净资产还高出八成——巴菲特愿意为Scott Fetzer的盈利能力支付高溢价，因为他买的是“经济护城河”而非账面资产：Kirby直销网络和World Book百科全书都是低资本开支的现金牛，这正是他日后总结的“以合理价格买伟大生意”的早期实践。

The first step in accounting for any premium paid is to adjust the carrying value of current assets to current values. In practice, this requirement usually does not affect receivables, which are routinely carried at current value, but often affects inventories. Because of a $22.9 million LIFO reserve and other accounting intricacies, Scott Fetzer's inventory account was carried at a $37.3 million discount from current value. So, making our first accounting move, we used $37.3 million of our $142.6 million premium to increase the carrying value of the inventory.

第一步：把流动资产调至现值，先用$37.3M溢价调高存货。由于$22.9M的LIFO（后进先出）储备及其他会计细节，Scott Fetzer存货账面值比现值低$37.3M。LIFO在通胀期会系统性低估存货价值——这是会计保守性的典型体现，也是收购整合中第一笔“看不见的资产”被摆上台面。

Assuming any premium is left after current assets are adjusted, the next step is to adjust fixed assets to current value. In our case, this adjustment also required a few accounting acrobatics relating to deferred taxes. Since this has been billed as a simplified discussion, I will skip the details and give you the bottom line: $68.0 million was added to fixed assets and $13.0 million was eliminated from deferred tax liabilities. After making this $81.0 million adjustment, we were left with $24.3 million of premium to allocate.

第二步：调高固定资产$68.0M，并消除$13.0M递延税负债，合计$81.0M。资产调高会同步产生额外的递延税负债，两者相互抵消，巴菲特称之为“会计杂技”。这轮调整后，溢价还剩$24.3M，将进入最后一步——商誉。注意每一步都在解释同一个问题：溢价不是“凭空消失的钱”，而是被分配到资产负债表的不同科目上。

Had our situation called for them two steps would next have been required: the adjustment of intangible assets other than Goodwill to current fair values, and the restatement of liabilities to current fair values, a requirement that typically affects only long-term debt and unfunded pension liabilities. In Scott Fetzer's case, however, neither of these steps was necessary.

另两步（无形资产调值与负债调值）在Scott Fetzer身上都不需要。这说明其资产结构简单：没有需要重估的无形资产，长期债务与未提拨养老金负债也无需调整。并购会计的复杂程度取决于标的资产结构——越复杂的公司，购买价格调整的“杂技”越多。

The final accounting adjustment we needed to make, after recording fair market values for all assets and liabilities, was the assignment of the residual premium to Goodwill (technically known as "excess of cost over the fair value of net assets acquired"). This residual amounted to $24.3 million. Thus, the balance sheet of Scott Fetzer immediately before the acquisition, which is summarized below in column O, was transformed by the purchase into the balance sheet shown in column N. In real terms, both balance sheets depict the same assets and liabilities - but, as you can see, certain figures differ significantly.

最后一步：剩余$24.3M全部记为商誉（“收购成本超过所获净资产公允价值之差额”）。于是收购前（O栏）的资产负债表被改写成收购后（N栏）。资产总额从$362.3M增至$492.0M——但真实资产分毫未变，变的只是会计标签。这是理解后面利润表差异的关键：资产写高→折旧摊销费用写高→利润写低，经济实质却完全不变。

The higher the asset figure, the higher the annual depreciation or amortization charge to earnings must be. It is important to understand that none of these newly-created accounting costs, totaling $11.6 million, are deductible for income tax purposes. The "new" Scott Fetzer pays exactly the same tax as the "old" Scott Fetzer would have, even though the GAAP earnings of the two entities differ greatly. And, in respect to operating earnings, that would be true in the future also. However, in the unlikely event that Scott Fetzer sells one of its businesses, the tax consequences to the "old" and "new" company might differ widely.

最重要的提醒：这$11.6M新增会计成本，一分钱都不能抵税。新、旧Scott Fetzer缴纳完全相同的税——GAAP利润差异不等于税负差异。这是理解“现金流”谬误的关键伏笔：许多人把会计摊销当成真实成本，但税后真实现金流并不因此减少。唯一的例外是极端情形：若未来出售旗下某业务，新旧口径的税务后果可能大不相同。

By the end of 1986 the difference between the net worth of the "old" and "new" Scott Fetzer had been reduced from $142.6 million to $131.0 million by means of the extra $11.6 million that was charged to earnings of the new entity. As the years go by, similar charges to earnings will cause most of the premium to disappear, and the two balance sheets will converge. However, the higher land values and most of the higher inventory values that were established on the new balance sheet will remain unless land is disposed of or inventory levels are further reduced.

随时间推移，溢价通过每年$11.6M的利润扣减逐渐消失，两栏资产负债表趋于收敛。但重估中“能留住的”部分——土地和大部分存货高估——会永久保留，除非土地被处置或库存进一步下降。这揭示了并购会计的时间维度：商誉摊销是逐年消耗的，而部分资产重估是一次性且不可逆的。

* * *

What does all this mean for owners? Did the shareholders of Berkshire buy a business that earned $40.2 million in 1986 or did they buy one earning $28.6 million? Were those $11.6 million of new charges a real economic cost to us? Should investors pay more for the stock of Company O than of Company N? And, if a business is worth some given multiple of earnings, was Scott Fetzer worth considerably more the day before we bought it than it was worth the following day?

连环追问：股东买的到底是赚$40.2M的生意，还是赚$28.6M的生意？$11.6M是真实经济成本吗？收购前一天的Scott Fetzer是否真的比收购后一天更值钱？这些问题直指会计数字与股东真实所得之间的鸿沟。巴菲特的答案：都不是——股东买的是这家企业未来产生可支配现金流的能力，会计口径的改变不可能让同一家企业在一夜之间贬值。

If we think through these questions, we can gain some insights about what may be called "owner earnings." These represent (a) reported earnings plus (b) depreciation, depletion, amortization, and certain other non-cash charges such as Company N's items (1) and (4) less

“所有者收益”（Owner Earnings）——巴菲特投资哲学中最重要的概念之一在此首次系统提出。公式为：(a)报告利润 ＋ (b)折旧、折耗、摊销及其他非现金费用（如N公司的(1)(4)项） － (c)为维持竞争地位所需的额外资本开支。全文的钥匙是“－(c)”：GAAP利润加上(b)并不等于所有者收益，因为大多数企业维持竞争力所需的资本开支大于折旧。这一概念与1980年的“穿透收益”（look-through earnings）互为表里：前者回答“生意真正赚多少钱”，后者回答“股东真正能分多少钱”。

Our owner-earnings equation does not yield the deceptively precise figures provided by GAAP, since The approach we have outlined produces "owner earnings" for Company O and Company N that are identical, which means valuations are also identical, just as common sense would tell you should be the case. This result is reached because the sum of (a) and (b) is the same in both columns O and N, and because

所有者收益公式不会给出GAAP那种“貌似精确”的数字——因为(c)只能靠判断估计。巴菲特坦承这是艺术而非科学，但“近似正确”远好于“精确错误”。O/N两栏的所有者收益完全相同→估值完全相同——常识早就告诉你答案：同一家公司不可能因会计处理不同而价值不同。那些因为“新口径利润更低”就以为收购吃亏的投资者，正是被会计数字绑架的典型。

And what do Charlie and I, as owners and managers, believe is the correct figure for the owner earnings of Scott Fetzer? Under current circumstances, we believe

巴菲特与芒格对Scott Fetzer所有者收益的估计：维持性资本开支与(b)大致相当。这意味着其所有者收益≈报告利润＋摊销等非现金项——罕见的好消息。Scott Fetzer是典型的“轻资本”生意：Kirby吸尘器与World Book百科全书维护性资本需求很低，这是巴菲特愿意支付高溢价的根本原因。但他在下一段立刻泼冷水：这种好事不常有。

That is obviously a happy state of affairs. But calculations of this sort usually do not provide such pleasant news. Most managers probably will acknowledge that they need to spend something more than (b) on their businesses over the longer term just to hold their ground in terms of both unit volume and competitive position. When this imperative exists - that is, when All of this points up the absurdity of the "cash flow" numbers that are often set forth in Wall Street reports. These numbers routinely include (a) plus (b) - but do not subtract

清醒的警告：这种“所有者收益≈(a)＋(b)”的好事不常有。多数企业为维持单位销量与竞争地位，长期必须花费比(b)更多的钱——当维持性资本开支大于折旧时，(a)＋(b)就高估了真实收益。这是区分“好生意”与“平庸生意”的核心测试：轻资产（如See's糖果，资本开支极少）vs 重资产（如伯克希尔早年深陷的纺织业，设备更新吞噬一切利润）。巴菲特在1979年信中对纺织业的反思，正是这一测试的早期版本。

"Cash Flow", true, may serve as a shorthand of some utility in descriptions of certain real estate businesses or other enterprises that make huge initial outlays and only tiny outlays thereafter. A company whose only holding is a bridge or an extremely long-lived gas field would be an example. But "cash flow" is meaningless in such businesses as manufacturing, retailing, extractive companies, and utilities because, for them,

“现金流”数字的荒谬之处：华尔街报告常列(a)＋(b)却不减去(c)。对需要持续大额资本开支的制造业、零售业、采掘业与公用事业，“现金流”是误导性的——它把维持生存的钱当成了可分配的钱。唯一适用“现金流”简写的是那些一次性大额投入、此后仅需微薄开支的生意，如一座桥或寿命极长的气田——巴菲特用这个例子反衬出：大多数企业根本不属于这一类。

Why, then, are "cash flow" numbers so popular today? In answer, we confess our cynicism: we believe these numbers are frequently used by marketers of businesses and securities in attempts to justify the unjustifiable (and thereby to sell what should be the unsalable). When (a) - that is, GAAP earnings - looks by itself inadequate to service debt of a junk bond or justify a foolish stock price, how convenient it becomes for salesmen to focus on (a) + (b). 为什么“现金流”数字如此流行？巴菲特罕见地坦承自己的愤世嫉俗。这些数字常被生意卖家和证券推销员用来“证明不该被证明的东西”——卖出本不该卖出的东西。当(a)（GAAP利润）不足以偿付垃圾债利息或支撑愚蠢的股价时，推销员就转向(a)＋(b)。这是对1980年代垃圾债券与杠杆收购狂潮的直接影射：那段时期华尔街用“EBITDA/现金流”讲故事，掩盖了高杠杆企业的真实偿债能力。巴菲特看穿了营销话术：不加回(c)就加上(b)，等于把“维持费”当“利润”。

To sum up: in the case of both Scott Fetzer and our other businesses, we feel that (b) on an historical-cost basis - i.e., with both amortization of intangibles and other purchase-price adjustments excluded - is quite close in amount to Questioning GAAP figures may seem impious to some. After all, what are we paying the accountants for if it is not to deliver us the "truth" about our business. But the accountants' job is to record, not to evaluate. The evaluation job falls to investors and managers.

总结：对Scott Fetzer及其他所有生意，历史成本口径下的(b)——剔除无形资产摊销与其他购买价格调整——与维持性资本开支相当接近。紧接着巴菲特抛出更尖锐的问题：质疑GAAP数字是否“大不敬”？答案是否定的：会计的职责是记录，不是评估——评估是投资者和管理者的工作。这句话是巴菲特会计哲学的总纲，也是他敢于公开批评“现金流”数字的理论依据。

Accounting numbers, of course, are the language of business and as such are of enormous help to anyone evaluating the worth of a business and tracking its progress. Charlie and I would be lost without these numbers: they invariably are the starting point for us in evaluating our own businesses and those of others. Managers and owners need to remember, however, that accounting is but an aid to business thinking, never a substitute for it.

结尾升华：会计数字是商业的语言，是评估生意的起点——但只是辅助工具，永远不能替代思考本身。没有这些数字，芒格和巴菲特也会迷失方向；但数字背后是经济实质，实质才是唯一真实的东西。这段话呼应了贯穿所有信件的主线：无论是1960年代的合伙基金时期，还是后来的保险浮存金与控股收购，巴菲特始终在用“生意人的眼光”审视会计数字，而非被数字牵着走。

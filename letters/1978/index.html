Berkshire Hathaway Letter — 1978

背景 1978年通胀飙升，美元贬值。巴菲特在信中强调'通胀是股票投资者最大的敌人'——这是他对抗通胀的经典论述。

BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC. To the Shareholders of Berkshire Hathaway Inc.: First, a few words about accounting. The merger with Diversified Retailing Company, Inc. at yearend adds two new complications in the presentation of our financial results. After the merger, our ownership of Blue Chip Stamps increased to approximately 58% and, therefore, the accounts of that company must be fully consolidated in the Balance Sheet and Statement of Earnings presentation of Berkshire. In previous reports, our share of the net earnings only of Blue Chip had been included as a single item on Berkshires Statement of Earnings, and there had been a similar one-line inclusion on our Balance Sheet of our share of their net assets.

"合并报表掩盖了经济现实。"巴菲特做了一个在 1970 年代极为罕见的决定：按业务分部报告利润，让股东看到每个业务的真实表现。传统合并报表把纺织、保险、糖果、银行的数字混在一起——看不清谁在赚钱谁在亏。巴菲特认为股东有权知道"钱从哪里来"。

1978 年的分部数据极其信息丰富：保险承保利润 $300 万（下降中），投资收入 $1970 万（增长中），纺织 $290 万（微利），See's 糖果 $1250 万（暴利），布法罗新闻 -$290 万（亏损），银行 $480 万（优秀）。对比一目了然：See's 和保险投资是利润引擎，纺织和布法罗新闻在拖后腿。这种透明度让股东能自己判断 Berkshire 的价值——而不是被一个笼统的 EPS 数字蒙蔽。

This full consolidation of sales, expenses, receivables, inventories, debt, etc. produces an aggregation of figures from many diverse businesses - textiles, insurance, candy, newspapers, trading stamps - with dramatically different economic characteristics. In some of these your ownership is 100% but, in those businesses which are owned by Blue Chip but fully consolidated, your ownership as a Berkshire shareholder is only 58%. (Ownership by others of the balance of these businesses is accounted for by the large minority interest item on the liability side of the Balance Sheet.) Such a grouping of Balance Sheet and Earnings items - some wholly owned, some partly owned - tends to obscure economic reality more than illuminate it. In fact, it represents a form of presentation that we never prepare for internal use during the year and which is of no value to us in any management activities.

"合并把不同经济特征的业务揉成一团，掩盖而非揭示经济现实。"合并后 Berkshire 对 Blue Chip 的持股升至约 58%，其全部收入、费用、应收、存货、负债被并入合并报表，但其中 42% 属于少数股东（计入负债侧的 minority interest 大项）。巴菲特直言：这种报表"我们内部从来不用，对管理毫无价值"——他坚持按业务分部看经济实质，而不是看合并后的笼统数字。

这段话为 1978 年首次完整的分部报告（Sources of Earnings 表）埋下伏笔：伯克希尔从此成为"信息的透明橱窗"，而非"数字的黑箱"。风险面：合并口径同时迫使 1977 年同期数字全部追溯重述，股东对比历史业绩时极易误读——巴菲特在信里反复提醒口径变化，这种"先讲清口径再讲业绩"的做法，本身就是给股东的一课。

For that reason, throughout the report we provide much separate financial information and commentary on the various segments of the business to help you evaluate Berkshires performance and prospects. Much of this segmented information is mandated by SEC disclosure rules and covered in Managements Discussion on pages 29 to 34. And in this letter we try to present to you a view of our various operating entities from the same perspective that we view them managerially.

"我们从管理层看业务的视角，向股东展示每一块业务。"1978 年是伯克希尔分部报告元年：SEC 要求的分部披露（年报第 29-34 页 Management's Discussion）只是底线，巴菲特主动走得更远——按纺织、保险、糖果、报纸、银行逐块给出税前/税后利润与归属份额。当时上市公司普遍只给一个笼统的 EPS 数字，伯克希尔的透明度是异类。

纵向看，这是 1965 年入主纺织厂后"看不透数字"的教训倒逼出来的：只有拆开看，股东才能自己发现 See's 是现金牛、布法罗新闻是亏损源，而不必依赖管理层的一家之言。风险面：透明也暴露短板（布法罗新闻 -$290 万、加州劳工险失控），但巴菲特选择相信股东能消化坏消息——后来几十年他始终坚持这个选择。 
A second complication arising from the merger is that the
1977 figures shown in this report are different from the 1977
figures shown in the report we mailed to you last year.
Accounting convention requires that when two entities such as
Diversified and Berkshire are merged, all financial data
subsequently must be presented as if the companies had been
merged at the time they were formed rather than just recently.
So the enclosed financial statements, in effect, pretend that in
1977 (and earlier years) the Diversified-Berkshire merger already
had taken place, even though the actual merger date was December
30, 1978.  This shifting base makes comparative commentary
confusing and, from time to time in our narrative report, we will
talk of figures and performance for Berkshire shareholders as
historically reported to you rather than as restated after the
Diversified merger.
"合并让 1977 年的数字变了样：会计上假装两家公司从来就是一家。"Diversified 与 Berkshire 于 1978 年 12 月 30 日完成合并，会计规则却要求追溯合并——把 1977 年（及更早）的报表"假装"早已合并。于是本年度报告里的 1977 年数字，与去年寄给股东的对不上。巴菲特特意说明：叙述部分他将按"历史上报给股东的口径"讨论业绩，而非重述后口径。

这是会计复杂性第一次大规模影响伯克希尔的报表呈现，也为日后反复出现的"重述 vs 原始口径"对比埋下伏笔。对普通投资者，这条的启示是：读财报先弄清口径，否则任何跨年对比都是空中楼阁。 
With that preamble it can be stated that, with or without
restated figures, 1978 was a good year.  Operating earnings,
exclusive of capital gains, at 19.4% of beginning shareholders
investment were within a fraction of our 1972 record.  While we
believe it is improper to include capital gains or losses in
evaluating the performance of a single year, they are an
important component of the longer term record.  Because of such
gains, Berkshires long-term growth in equity per share has been
greater than would be indicated by compounding the returns from
operating earnings that we have reported annually.
"1978 年经营利润率 19.4%，逼近 1972 年的历史纪录。"巴菲特再次用"期初股东权益回报率"（ROE）而非每股收益衡量业绩——这是他在 1977 年信中确立的基准。19.4% 意味着每 100 美元期初权益赚回 19.4 美元，与 1972 年纪录仅差毫厘。他还划清一条原则：评价单年业绩不应计入资本利得，但长期记录必须计入——资本利得让每股净值的长期复合增速高于经营收益的简单复利。

风险面紧随其后：他立刻警告这个数字不可持续——保险周期已在 1979 年掉头，以 ROE 衡量的经营回报今年几乎必然回落；只有以美元计的绝对利润会因权益基数变大而继续增长。"相对回报降、绝对利润升"这对组合，是巴菲特给股东的预期管理范本。 
For example, over the last three years - generally a bonanza
period for the insurance industry, our largest profit producer -
Berkshires per share net worth virtually has doubled, thereby
compounding at about 25% annually through a combination of good
operating earnings and fairly substantial capital gains.  Neither
this 25% equity gain from all sources nor the 19.4% equity gain
from operating earnings in 1978 is sustainable.  The insurance
cycle has turned downward in 1979, and it is almost certain that
operating earnings measured by return on equity will fall this
year.  However, operating earnings measured in dollars are likely
to increase on the much larger shareholders equity now employed
in the business.
"三年净值翻倍、年复合约 25%——但不可持续。"1976-1978 是保险业的大丰收期，叠加可观的资本利得，伯克希尔每股净值三年翻倍。巴菲特罕见地主动泼冷水：保险周期 1979 年已掉头，以 ROE 衡量的经营收益今年几乎必然下滑；只有以美元计的绝对利润会因权益基数变大而继续增长。

这是"顺风/逆风"思维的延续：1977 年他讲纺织是逆风行业、保险是顺风行业，1978 年则示范如何对待顺风——在行业景气顶点保持清醒，不把周期性高点当永久状态。当时市场共识恰恰相反：保险股热潮中，投资者普遍把高承保利润外推为常态，为 1979-1980 年的行业亏损埋下伏笔。 
In contrast to this cautious view about near term return
from operations, we are optimistic about prospects for long term
return from major equity investments held by our insurance
companies.  We make no attempt to predict how security markets
will behave; successfully forecasting short term stock price
movements is something we think neither we nor anyone else can
do.  In the longer run, however, we feel that many of our major
equity holdings are going to be worth considerably more money
than we paid, and that investment gains will add significantly to
the operating returns of the insurance group.
"我们从不预测股市短期走势——我们认为没人能做到。"巴菲特明确拒绝预测短期股价：既不认为自己、也不认为任何人有此能力。但长期看，他确信保险组合里"许多"主要持股将值远高于买入价，投资利得会显著增厚保险业务的经营回报。

这条"短期不可知、长期可判断"的分界线贯穿他此后全部生涯：买入时看的是企业未来多年的盈利能力，而非下季度股价。风险面：长期判断同样可能错——所以他用的是"许多"而非"全部"，并强调这是"感觉"（feel）而非断言。 
Sources of Earnings
To give you a better picture of just where Berkshires
earnings are produced, we show below a table which requires a
little explanation.  Berkshire owns close to 58% of Blue Chip
which, in addition to 100% ownership of several businesses, owns
80% of Wesco Financial Corporation.  Thus, Berkshires equity in
Wescos earnings is about 46%.  In aggregate, businesses that we
control have about 7,000 full-time employees and generate
revenues of over $500 million.
"我们控制的企业约 7,000 名员工、营收超 5 亿美元——第一次把家底盘清楚。"股权结构层层嵌套：Berkshire 持 Blue Chip 约 58%，Blue Chip 又持 Wesco 80%，因此 Berkshire 对 Wesco 利润的实际权益约 46%。表格三列分别为：业务税前总利润、归属 Berkshire 的税前份额、税后份额——既看整体，也看"属于我们的那部分"。

巴菲特开宗明义：因会计与税务复杂性，表内数字只能视为"近似值"，不可当作圣旨——他一贯反对用报表的虚假精确假装确定性。这与他合伙基金时期"宁可模糊的正确，不要精确的错误"一脉相承。

1978 年净利表：经营利润 $5,240 万 + 已实现证券利得 $1,380 万 = 总额 $6,620 万；归属 Berkshire 的税后利润 $3,920 万。表中三个看点：其一，保险投资收入 $1,970 万（上年 $1,280 万，+54%），已是承保利润 $300 万（上年 $580 万，腰斩）的 6 倍多——"浮存金投资"引擎正式接棒，这是伯克希尔商业模式演化的关键一年；其二，See's 糖果税前 $1,250 万继续稳坐最大现金牛；其三，布法罗新闻从上年 +$75 万恶化到 -$290 万，纺织 $290 万微利，伊利诺伊银行 $480 万稳步增长。

承保利润腰斩而总投资收入大增，正是巴菲特在信首预告的"周期下行但绝对利润增长"的预演。保险、媒体双引擎的持仓结构，让伯克希尔对承保周期的敏感度逐年下降。

requirements of their own.  Later in this report we are
reproducing the narrative reports of the principal executives of
both companies, describing their 1978 operations.  Some of the
figures they utilize will not match to the penny the ones we use
in this report, again because of accounting and tax complexities.
But their comments should be helpful to you in understanding the
underlying economic characteristics of these important partly-
owned businesses.  A copy of the full annual report of either
company will be mailed to any shareholder of Berkshire upon
request to Mr. Robert H. Bird for Blue Chips Stamps, 5801 South

"Blue Chip 与 Wesco 是上市公司，各有各的披露义务。"巴菲特建议股东直接向两家公司索取完整年报，并引用其高管对 1978 年经营的自述——因为"他们的数字与我们的对不上分毫，又是会计与税务复杂性所致"。这是他一贯的做法：不垄断信息，鼓励股东交叉验证。

背后的事实是：伯克希尔对这两家"控股但非全资"，管理层各自对自家股东负责。58% 的持股比例意味着伯克希尔既是老板又是外部股东——这种"部分控股"结构在日后 Wesco（80% 走向全资）与 Blue Chip 的演化中反复出现。 
Textiles
Earnings of $1.3 million in 1978, while much improved from
1977, still represent a low return on the $17 million of capital
employed in this business.  Textile plant and equipment are on
the books for a very small fraction of what it would cost to
replace such equipment today.  And, despite the age of the
equipment, much of it is functionally similar to new equipment
being installed by the industry.  But despite this bargain cost
of fixed assets, capital turnover is relatively low reflecting
required high investment levels in receivables and inventory
compared to sales.  Slow capital turnover, coupled with low
profit margins on sales, inevitably produces inadequate returns
on capital.  Obvious approaches to improved profit margins
involve differentiation of product, lowered manufacturing costs
through more efficient equipment or better utilization of people,
redirection toward fabrics enjoying stronger market trends, etc.
Our management is diligent in pursuing such objectives.  The
problem, of course, is that our competitors are just as
diligently doing the same thing.
"纺织赚 $130 万，但在 $1,700 万资本上仍是低回报。"巴菲特点破纺织业的核心矛盾：设备账面价值远低于重置成本（看似"便宜"），但资本周转慢——应收与库存占用高——叠加利润率低，资本回报必然不足。管理层再勤奋也没用："问题在于，我们的竞争对手同样勤奋。"

这是"重资产、无差异化行业"的典型解剖：账面便宜不是真便宜，资产周转率与利润率才是。1977 年他已用纺织论证"顺风/逆风"框架，1978 年继续用同一案例深化——纺织厂最终在 1985 年被关闭，但在 1978 年，这一切尚无定数，他仍在信中为继续经营辩护。

The textile industry illustrates in textbook style how producers of relatively undifferentiated goods in capital intensive businesses must earn inadequate returns except under conditions of tight supply or real shortage. As long as excess productive capacity exists, prices tend to reflect direct operating costs rather than capital employed. Such a supply- excess condition appears likely to prevail most of the time in the textile industry, and our expectations are for profits of relatively modest amounts in relation to capital.

"教科书级"的商品经济学的定义。这段话是巴菲特对"差行业"最精炼的概括：(1) 产品无差异化→无法定价；(2) 资本密集→需要大量投入维持竞争力；(3) 产能过剩→价格只反映运营成本不反映资本回报。三个条件叠加 = 投资者永远只能获得微薄回报，除非出现供给短缺。

这段话的价值不在于解释纺织——而在于提供了一个通用的"差行业识别框架"。后来巴菲特用它来排除航空业（同样无差异化+资本密集+产能过剩）、大部分大宗商品制造业。相反，好行业的特征是：(1) 产品有差异化或品牌；(2) 轻资产；(3) 供给受限或有壁垒。See's 糖果完美符合好行业的标准——这正是巴菲特在 1972 年收购 See's 后逐步领悟的。"先看行业再看公司"——这个看似简单的原则，巴菲特用了 13 年（1965-1978）才从纺织的痛苦中学到。 
We hope we dont get into too many more businesses with such
tough economic characteristics.  But, as we have stated before:
(1) our textile businesses are very important employers in their
communities, (2) management has been straightforward in reporting
on problems and energetic in attacking them, (3) labor has been
cooperative and understanding in facing our common problems, and
(4) the business should average modest cash returns relative to
investment.  As long as these conditions prevail - and we expect
that they will - we intend to continue to support our textile
business despite more attractive alternative uses for capital.
"我们不想再进太多这种经济特性的行业。"但巴菲特给出继续留在纺织业的四条理由：社区重要雇主、管理层坦诚且卖力、劳工配合、以及"平均仅能获得适度的现金回报"。他明说：即使资本有更吸引人的去处，只要这些条件成立，就继续支持纺织。

这是"股东利益 vs 社会责任"的罕见坦诚时刻：理性上该退出，伯克希尔却选择承担社区责任，同时用"modest returns"的底线预期划清边界——既不让情怀无限吞噬资本，也不做冷血的裁员式退出。这种平衡后来成为伯克希尔处理夕阳业务的默认姿态。 
Insurance Underwriting
The number one contributor to Berkshires overall excellent
results in 1978 was the segment of National Indemnity Companys
insurance operation run by Phil Liesche.  On about $90 million of
earned premiums, an underwriting profit of approximately $11
million was realized, a truly extraordinary achievement even
against the background of excellent industry conditions.  Under
Phils leadership, with outstanding assistance by Roland Miller
in Underwriting and Bill Lyons in Claims, this segment of
National Indemnity (including National Fire and Marine Insurance
Company, which operates as a running mate) had one of its best
years in a long history of performances which, in aggregate, far
outshine those of the industry.  Present successes reflect credit
not only upon present managers, but equally upon the business
talents of Jack Ringwalt, founder of National Indemnity, whose
operating philosophy remains etched upon the company.
"Phil Liesche 的部门以约 $9,000 万保费赚得约 $1,100 万承保利润——行业顶尖的非凡成就。"即便在行业大年背景下，这仍是"真正非凡的成就"。巴菲特把功劳同时归于现任管理层与创始人 Jack Ringwalt 留下的经营哲学——National Indemnity 的承保纪律是"刻在公司骨子里"的。

这条线索从 1967 年买入延续至今：巴菲特反复强调保险业务的成败在人——1977 年他专门写过"保险公司卖的是承诺，没有品牌护城河，人就是一切"。1978 年的"非凡"要由未来数年的承保记录来检验——周期拐点已近，但巴菲特显然愿意押注于人。

Home and Automobile Insurance Company had its best year since John Seward stepped in and straightened things out in 1975. Its results are combined in this report with those of Phil Liesches operation under the insurance category entitled Specialized Auto and General Liability.

"Home and Automobile 迎来 John Seward 1975 年整顿以来的最佳一年。"1974-75 年行业危机中，Home and Auto 的承保曾"红墨水泛滥"、濒临倒闭，Seward 是危机中提拔的"战场指挥官"。1978 年其业绩并入 Phil Liesche 部门，归入专业车险与一般责任险类别。

这是伯克希尔保险板块"人治"的又一例证：同一家公司，换对人就从濒死到最佳。市场共识看牌照与规模，巴菲特看的是管理者的处置能力。

Workers Compensation was a mixed bag in 1978. In its first year as a subsidiary, Cypress Insurance Company, managed by Milt Thornton, turned in outstanding results. The workers compensation line can cause large underwriting losses when rapid inflation interacts with changing social concepts, but Milt has a cautious and highly professional staff to cope with these problems. His performance in 1978 has reinforced our very good feelings about this purchase.

"劳工险是通胀的放大器——但 Milt Thornton 的 Cypress 第一年就交出优秀成绩。"巴菲特点出劳工险的特殊风险："快速通胀碰上社会观念变迁"（赔付标准被法院与社会认知不断放宽），可以造成巨大承保亏损——这正是加州劳工险部门此前失控的原因。Milt 的谨慎与专业，让这笔 1977 年底的收购（约 $1,250 万保费规模）得到验证。

注意巴菲特的风险框架：他不只看当期业绩，更看业务对通胀的暴露度。劳工险=通胀敏感型业务，需要特别谨慎的管理者来对冲。

Frank DeNardo came with us in the spring of 1978 to straighten out National Indemnitys California Workers Compensation business which, up to that point, had been a disaster. Frank has the experience and intellect needed to correct the major problems of the Los Angeles office. Our volume in this department now is running only about 25% of what it was eighteen months ago, and early indications are that Frank is making good progress.

"Frank DeNardo 1978 年春入职，收拾加州劳工险这个烂摊子。"巴菲特直言该业务"至今一直是场灾难"：部门规模已缩减到 18 个月前的 25%——先砍量、再治本。他看中的是 Frank 的经验与头脑，以及"早期迹象显示进展良好"。

这是伯克希尔保险板块"收缩式修复"的典型：承认错误、主动缩量、换将重整。市场常把"增长"当成功指标，巴菲特把"止血"也算作进步——1979-1980 年该部门逐步恢复，验证了这一耐心。

George Youngs reinsurance department continues to produce very large sums for investment relative to premium volume, and thus gives us reasonably satisfactory overall results. However, underwriting results still are not what they should be and can be. It is very easy to fool yourself regarding underwriting results in reinsurance (particularly in casualty lines involving long delays in settlement), and we believe this situation prevails with many of our competitors. Unfortunately, self- delusion in company reserving almost always leads to inadequate industry rate levels. If major factors in the market dont know their true costs, the competitive fall-out hits all - even those with adequate cost knowledge. George is quite willing to reduce volume significantly, if needed, to achieve satisfactory underwriting, and we have a great deal of confidence in the long term soundness of this business under his direction.

"再保险最容易自欺：赔案结算拖延多年，账面承保结果可以长期失真。"巴菲特点破再保险行业的系统性风险：准备金计提（reserving）的自欺欺人"几乎必然导致行业费率水平不足"——因为主要玩家不知道自己的真实成本，竞争性"fall-out"会波及所有人，包括成本控制良好者。George Young 愿意为承保质量大幅削减规模，正是对这种行业病的解药。

这段话是 1990-2000 年代再保险巨灾亏损的预言式警告：当行业普遍低估风险时，谨慎者也被拖下水。市场共识追逐再保险的"浮存金杠杆"，巴菲特坚持"承保纪律优先于规模"。

The homestate operation was disappointing in 1978. Our unsatisfactory underwriting, even though partially explained by an unusual incidence of Midwestern storms, is particularly worrisome against the backdrop of very favorable industry results in the conventional lines written by our homestate group. We have confidence in John Ringwalts ability to correct this situation. The bright spot in the group was the performance of Kansas Fire and Casualty in its first full year of business. Under Floyd Taylor, this subsidiary got off to a truly remarkable start. Of course, it takes at least several years to evaluate underwriting results, but the early signs are encouraging and Floyds operation achieved the best loss ratio among the homestate companies in 1978.

"homestate 板块 1978 年令人失望——但 Kansas Fire and Casualty 首年惊艳。"中西部异常风暴只能部分解释承保不佳；更令巴菲特警惕的是：行业大好之年，自家传统险种却跑输。John Ringwalt（创始人 Jack 之子）被授权纠偏。亮点是 Floyd Taylor 执掌的 Kansas Fire and Casualty——首个完整年度就拿下全组最低赔付率。

巴菲特反复提醒："承保结果至少要几年才能评估"，首年数据只算早期信号。这是他一贯的"长周期评估"纪律：既不因单年好而欢呼，也不因单年差而仓促换人。 
Although some segments were disappointing, overall our
insurance operation had an excellent year.  But of course we
should expect a good year when the industry is flying high, as in
1978.  It is a virtual certainty that in 1979 the combined ratio
(see definition on page 31) for the industry will move up at
least a few points, perhaps enough to throw the industry as a
whole into an underwriting loss position.  For example, in the
auto lines - by far the most important area for the industry and
for us - CPI figures indicate rates overall were only 3% higher
in January 1979 than a year ago.  But the items that make up loss
costs - auto repair and medical care costs - were up over 9%.
How different than yearend 1976 when rates had advanced over 22%
in the preceding twelve months, but costs were up 8%.
"1979 年行业综合成本率几乎必然上升——费率只涨 3%，成本却涨 9%。"巴菲特用一组数据预判周期拐点：1979 年 1 月车险费率同比仅 +3%，而构成赔付成本的修车与医疗费用涨超 9%；对比 1976 年底费率曾 +22%、成本 +8%。费率与成本增速错位，承保利润必然被压缩，行业可能整体陷入承保亏损。

这是"保险周期"分析的经典范式：把费率周期与通胀周期对照，而非看单年结果。1977 年他预告"钟摆开始回摆"，1978 年给出具体数字坐实拐点。风险面：他也承认判断可能错——"每一家保险管理层都觉得自己会跑赢同行，总有人要失望"。 
Margins will remain steady only if rates rise as fast as
costs.  This assuredly will not be the case in 1979, and
conditions probably will worsen in 1980.  Our present thinking is
that our underwriting performance relative to the industry will
improve somewhat in 1979, but every other insurance management
probably views its relative prospects with similar optimism -
someone is going to be disappointed.  Even if we do improve
relative to others, we may well have a higher combined ratio and
lower underwriting profits in 1979 than we achieved last year.
"费率必须与成本同步上涨，利润率才守得住——1979 年显然不会。"巴菲特判断 1979 年承保相对行业的排名或可改善，但绝对水平（综合成本率）很可能上升、承保利润下降——"有人要失望"。这是他对"相对 vs 绝对"的清醒：不承诺跑赢市场的绝对收益，只承诺纪律与相对优势。

纵向看，1979-1980 年伯克希尔果然出现承保亏损年份，验证了这一预判；而"相对改善、绝对回落"的表述方式，也成为他日后管理股东预期的标准句式。

We continue to look for ways to expand our insurance operation. But your reaction to this intent should not be unrestrained joy. Some of our expansion efforts - largely initiated by your Chairman have been lackluster, others have been expensive failures. We entered the business in 1967 through purchase of the segment which Phil Liesche now manages, and it still remains, by a large margin, the best portion of our insurance business. It is not easy to buy a good insurance business, but our experience has been that it is easier to buy one than create one. However, we will continue to try both approaches, since the rewards for success in this field can be exceptional.

"买一家好保险公司比创办一家容易——但我们两条路都试。"巴菲特自曝家丑：扩张努力中"有些平庸、有些是昂贵的失败"，并坦然归咎于"董事长（自己）发起"。1967 年买入、现由 Phil Liesche 掌管的部门，至今仍是保险业务中最好的部分。

市场共识是"并购=增长=好事"，巴菲特却把并购的失败率摊开给股东看——收购 See's（1972）的成功与若干平庸收购并存，说明他的收购哲学是"宁缺毋滥、承认试错成本"。"买比造容易"这句话，后来成为他评估一切扩张方案的标尺。

Insurance Investments

We confess considerable optimism regarding our insurance equity investments. Of course, our enthusiasm for stocks is not unconditional. Under some circumstances, common stock investments by insurers make very little sense.

"对保险股票投资的乐观不是无条件的。"巴菲特先亮明热情，再设前提：某些情形下，保险公司买股票"毫无意义"。这句话是下一段"选股四条件"的引子——他的乐观建立在严格筛选之上，而非对股市整体的看多。

1978 年底道指仅 805 点、市场情绪低迷，养老金入市比例创纪录新低，巴菲特却说"价值投资者的美妙时期"——与当时"股票已死"的共识形成鲜明反差。他不是因为乐观才买，而是因为便宜才买。 
We get excited enough to commit a big percentage of
insurance company net worth to equities only when we find (1)
businesses we can understand, (2) with favorable long-term
prospects, (3) operated by honest and competent people, and (4)
priced very attractively.  We usually can identify a small number
of potential investments meeting requirements (1), (2) and (3),
but (4) often prevents action.  For example, in 1971 our total
common stock position at Berkshires insurance subsidiaries
amounted to only $10.7 million at cost, and $11.7 million at
market.  There were equities of identifiably excellent companies
available - but very few at interesting prices. (An irresistible
footnote: in 1971, pension fund managers invested a record 122%
of net funds available in equities - at full prices they couldnt
buy enough of them.  In 1974, after the bottom had fallen out,
they committed a then record low of 21% to stocks.)
"只有四个条件同时满足，我们才愿意把保险公司的净资产大比例押进股票：(1) 看得懂；(2) 长期前景好；(3) 诚实能干的人经营；(4) 价格极具吸引力。"巴菲特点出常态：前三条同时满足的机会常有，第四条"价格"常常挡住行动——1971 年保险子公司股票仓位成本仅 $1,070 万（市值 $1,170 万），因为"好公司很多，便宜的好公司很少"。

旁注极具讽刺：1971 年养老金经理把净流入资金的 122% 投入股票（贵到买不够），1974 年崩盘后只投 21%——机构在顶部贪婪、底部恐惧，巴菲特反其道而行。这是"选股四条件"的完整表述（four-criteria，1977 年已有雏形），此后成为伯克希尔投资的公开公式。 
The past few years have been a different story for us.  At
the end of 1975 our insurance subsidiaries held common equities
with a market value exactly equal to cost of $39.3 million.  At
the end of 1978 this position had been increased to equities
(including a convertible preferred) with a cost of $129.1 million
and a market value of $216.5 million.  During the intervening
three years we also had realized pre-tax gains from common
equities of approximately $24.7 million.  Therefore, our overall
unrealized and realized pre-tax gains in equities for the three
year period came to approximately $112 million.  During this same
interval the Dow-Jones Industrial Average declined from 852 to
805.  It was a marvelous period for the value-oriented equity
buyer.
"三年间保险持股成本从 $3,930 万增至 $1.291 亿、市值 $2.165 亿，浮盈与已实现利得合计约 $1.12 亿——而道指同期从 852 跌到 805。"这是"市场下跌+价值买入"策略的实绩证明：指数没涨，组合翻了几倍。1978 年新建仓 SAFECO、增持华盛顿邮报等，都是在"无人问津"的价位完成的。

"对价值投资者而言，这是一个美妙的时期"——潜台词是：市场越冷，伯克希尔越受益。风险面：账面浮盈随市波动（1974 年曾出现 $1,700 万浮亏），巴菲特提醒"任何单日价格都不该被太当真"，组合的胜负要由企业多年的经营结果来判定。

We continue to find for our insurance portfolios small portions of really outstanding businesses that are available, through the auction pricing mechanism of security markets, at prices dramatically cheaper than the valuations inferior businesses command on negotiated sales.

"通过股市的拍卖定价机制，我们总能买到比协议收购价便宜得多的优秀企业碎片。"巴菲特点出二级市场的独特功能：它为优秀企业提供"打折入场券"，同一家企业在谈判桌上要价常是股价的两倍。不控股、不竞购，只等在拍卖市场上捡便宜——这是"不控股优势"的另一个侧面（延续 1977 年 Capital Cities 案例）。

市场共识追逐"控制权溢价"的并购故事，巴菲特却说：优秀的生意，买碎片比买整家更划算。风险面：碎片没有控制权，无法干预资本配置——他用"只买管理层记录一流的企业"来对冲这个缺陷。 
This program of acquisition of small fractions of businesses
(common stocks) at bargain prices, for which little enthusiasm
exists, contrasts sharply with general corporate acquisition
activity, for which much enthusiasm exists.  It seems quite clear
to us that either corporations are making very significant
mistakes in purchasing entire businesses at prices prevailing in
negotiated transactions and takeover bids, or that we eventually
are going to make considerable sums of money buying small
portions of such businesses at the greatly discounted valuations
prevailing in the stock market. (A second footnote: in 1978
pension managers, a group that logically should maintain the
longest of investment perspectives, put only 9% of net available
funds into equities - breaking the record low figure set in 1974
and tied in 1977.)
"要么企业界在协议收购中犯了严重错误，要么我们买股票碎片会赚大钱——二选一。"巴菲特用归谬法：整家收购的溢价与二级市场的折价不可能同时正确。1978 年养老金经理只把净流入的 9% 投入股票（打破 1974 年 21% 的纪录低点）——"本该眼光最长的机构"却在底部离场。

两条脚注（1971 年 122%、1978 年 9%）构成他日后反复引用的"机构情绪钟摆"证据：别人的恐慌就是伯克希尔的粮仓。风险面：若市场长期低迷，浮盈会缩水——但巴菲特买的是企业盈利而非股价，账面波动被他定义为"机会而非风险"。

We are not concerned with whether the market quickly revalues upward securities that we believe are selling at bargain prices. In fact, we prefer just the opposite since, in most years, we expect to have funds available to be a net buyer of securities. And consistent attractive purchasing is likely to prove to be of more eventual benefit to us than any selling opportunities provided by a short-term run up in stock prices to levels at which we are unwilling to continue buying.

"我们不关心市场何时重新定价——事实上我们更喜欢它别涨。"因为多数年份伯克希尔是净买入方：股价越低，同样的钱能买到更多好生意。持续低价买入的长期收益，将超过短期股价上涨带来的卖出机会。

这彻底颠覆散户"买入=盼涨"的心理。1970 年代巴菲特反复强调自己是"永久净买家"——股价下跌等于打折促销。风险面：这个逻辑的前提是手里始终有现金（保险浮存金）；若浮存金收缩或机会成本上升，"喜欢下跌"就只是漂亮话。

Our policy is to concentrate holdings. We try to avoid buying a little of this or that when we are only lukewarm about the business or its price. When we are convinced as to attractiveness, we believe in buying worthwhile amounts.

"我们集中持仓：不温不火就不买，看准了就下重注。"巴菲特反对"什么都买一点"的撒胡椒面策略——对不喜欢的生意或价格，宁可不买；一旦确信吸引力，就买"值得的规模"。

纵向看，这套集中逻辑在 1980 年代华盛顿邮报、GEICO、可口可乐的重仓上继续放大。集中持仓意味着波动更大——风险面巴菲特用"确信度"对冲，但他也提醒：普通投资者若无同等研究深度，盲目模仿集中持仓的风险极高。

In some cases our indirect interest in earning power is becoming quite substantial. For example, note our holdings of 953,750 shares of SAFECO Corp. SAFECO probably is the best run large property and casualty insurance company in the United States. Their underwriting abilities are simply superb, their loss reserving is conservative, and their investment policies make great sense.

"SAFECO 可能是美国经营最好的大型财险公司。"巴菲特对 SAFECO 的赞美几乎溢出纸面：承保能力"无可挑剔"、准备金计提保守、投资政策合理。这是 1978 年新建的最大仓位（95.4 万股，成本约 $2,390 万）。

市场共识追逐"讲故事"的成长股，巴菲特却重仓一家低调的西雅图财险公司——他买的是经营质量，不是市场热度。这笔买入是否明智，要由未来多年的承保与投资收益来检验；不控股的仓位尤其如此。 
SAFECO is a much better insurance operation than our own
(although we believe certain segments of ours are much better
than average), is better than one we could develop and,
similarly, is far better than any in which we might negotiate
purchase of a controlling interest.  Yet our purchase of SAFECO
was made at substantially under book value.  We paid less than
100 cents on the dollar for the best company in the business,
when far more than 100 cents on the dollar is being paid for
mediocre companies in corporate transactions.  And there is no
way to start a new operation - with necessarily uncertain
prospects - at less than 100 cents on the dollar.
"我们用不到 100 美分买了全行业最好的公司——而平庸公司在并购市场要价远高于 100 美分。"巴菲特把 SAFECO 的三重优势讲透：比我们自己的保险业务好、比我们能创办的好、比我们能协议收购的任何一家都好；而且买入价低于账面价值。二级市场的折价，让"最好的公司"以"最便宜的价格"成交。

这就是"拍卖机制 vs 谈判机制"的定价差：谈判桌上买平庸公司要溢价，拍卖市场上买优秀公司打折扣。风险面：不控股意味着 SAFECO 的留存收益如何再投资，巴菲特无法干预——他的信任依据是"管理层记录"。

Of course, with a minor interest we do not have the right to direct or even influence management policies of SAFECO. But why should we wish to do this? The record would indicate that they do a better job of managing their operations than we could do ourselves. While there may be less excitement and prestige in sitting back and letting others do the work, we think that is all one loses by accepting a passive participation in excellent management. Because, quite clearly, if one controlled a company run as well as SAFECO, the proper policy also would be to sit back and let management do its job.

"持少数股权无法指挥 SAFECO 的管理——但为什么要指挥？"巴菲特反问：一家比自己管得更好的公司，股东的正确姿势就是坐享其成。"放弃的只是兴奋感和面子，得到的是一流管理的全部收益。"即使控股一家 SAFECO 这样的公司，正确做法同样是让管理层放手干。

这是"不控股的控股"（non-control-advantage）的经典表述，1977 年 Capital Cities 案例的延续：被动参与优秀管理，胜过主动指挥平庸管理。市场共识迷信控制权，巴菲特证明放弃控制权反而可能获得更好的结果。

Earnings attributable to the shares of SAFECO owned by Berkshire at yearend amounted to $6.1 million during 1978, but only the dividends received (about 18% of earnings) are reflected in our operating earnings. We believe the balance, although not reportable, to be just as real in terms of eventual benefit to us as the amount distributed. In fact, SAFECOs retained earnings (or those of other well-run companies if they have opportunities to employ additional capital advantageously) may well eventually have a value to shareholders greater than 100 cents on the dollar.

"SAFECO 1978 年归属我们的利润 $610 万，但只有约 18% 的分红计入经营利润。"巴菲特提出"未分配收益同样真实"：留存部分虽不上报表，最终利益同样归属股东——优秀公司若能以有吸引力的回报再投资，留存收益的未来价值可能"超过 100 美分"。

这是"穿透收益"（look-through earnings）思想的雏形，1980 年信中他将正式提出。市场只盯着报表利润，巴菲特盯着企业整体的盈利能力——这个视角差，正是他与华尔街之间最深的鸿沟之一。

We are not at all unhappy when our wholly-owned businesses retain all of their earnings if they can utilize internally those funds at attractive rates. Why should we feel differently about retention of earnings by companies in which we hold small equity interests, but where the record indicates even better prospects for profitable employment of capital? (This proposition cuts the other way, of course, in industries with low capital requirements, or if management has a record of plowing capital into projects of low profitability; then earnings should be paid out or used to repurchase shares - often by far the most attractive option for capital utilization.)

"全资子公司留存利润我们乐见其成——对持股很少的好公司，凭什么双标？"只要内部再投资回报有吸引力，留存就是对的。巴菲特同时给出反例：低资本需求行业、或管理层有乱投资前科时，利润应分红或回购——"回购往往是资本运用中最有吸引力的选项"。

这段话首次把"留存 vs 分红 vs 回购"的决策框架完整写出，比 1984 年那封著名的讨论留存收益的信早了六年。市场共识认为"分红才是回报"，巴菲特认为"资本配置效率才是回报"。

The aggregate level of such retained earnings attributable to our equity interests in fine companies is becoming quite substantial. It does not enter into our reported operating earnings, but we feel it well may have equal long-term significance to our shareholders. Our hope is that conditions continue to prevail in securities markets which allow our insurance companies to buy large amounts of underlying earning power for relatively modest outlays. At some point market conditions undoubtedly will again preclude such bargain buying but, in the meantime, we will try to make the most of opportunities.

"归属我们的留存收益规模已相当可观——虽不计入报表利润，长期意义可能同等重大。"巴菲特希望市场继续提供"以适中价格买入大量盈利能力"的机会，但也清醒预告：总有一天市场条件会再次让这种便宜买卖消失——"在那之前，我们会尽量利用机会"。

这是对"市场永远有机会"的否定：机会窗口是周期性的。1972 年（找不到便宜货）与 1974-1978（遍地便宜货）的交替，就是他亲历的证据——后来 1999 年科技泡沫、2021 年疯牛，他都在重复这同一句话。

Banking

Under Gene Abegg and Pete Jeffrey, the Illinois National Bank and Trust Company in Rockford continues to establish new records. Last years earnings amounted to approximately 2.1% of average assets, about three times the level averaged by major banks. In our opinion, this extraordinary level of earnings is being achieved while maintaining significantly less asset risk than prevails at most of the larger banks.

"伊利诺伊银行资产收益率约 2.1%——大型银行平均水平的 3 倍，且风险更低。"Gene Abegg 与 Pete Jeffrey 管理的这家 Rockford 银行持续刷新纪录。巴菲特特意强调：高收益不是靠高风险换来的——"资产风险显著低于大多数大银行"。

银行是 1970 年代伯克希尔组合里最被低估的资产：1977 年信中他就用"3 倍于大银行的资产收益率"作对比。市场共识追逐大银行的规模故事，巴菲特偏好小银行的效率与保守。

We purchased the Illinois National Bank in March 1969. It was a first-class operation then, just as it had been ever since Gene Abegg opened the doors in 1931. Since 1968, consumer time deposits have quadrupled, net income has tripled and trust department income has more than doubled, while costs have been closely controlled.

"1969 年 3 月买入伊利诺伊银行时它就是一流企业——1931 年开业至今一贯如此。"十年数据：消费定期存款翻两番、净利润增两倍、信托收入翻倍以上，而成本控制严格。巴菲特用长周期数据证明：好银行是"时间的朋友"。

注意收购时点：1969 年是市场狂热期（漂亮五十泡沫），巴菲特却买了一家不起眼的小银行——他不追热点，只买价格合理的好生意。Gene Abegg 从 1931 年开业一直管理到银行分拆（1980），是巴菲特"终身经理人"理念的早期样本。

Our experience has been that the manager of an already high- cost operation frequently is uncommonly resourceful in finding new ways to add to overhead, while the manager of a tightly-run operation usually continues to find additional methods to curtail costs, even when his costs are already well below those of his competitors. No one has demonstrated this latter ability better than Gene Abegg.

"高成本公司的经理总擅长发明新开销；低成本公司的经理总在继续压缩成本。"巴菲特总结成本纪律的"马太效应"——并以 Gene Abegg 为例：成本已低于同行，仍在寻找新的削减方法。

这是"管理文化决定成本"的洞见，与 1980 年代 B 夫人（内布拉斯加家具城）的低成本文化一脉相承。市场常把成本控制当作一次性项目，巴菲特视为持续的性格特征——所以他选人时先看"性格"。 
We are required to divest our bank by December 31, 1980.
The most likely approach is to spin it off to Berkshire
shareholders some time in the second half of 1980.
"1980 年 12 月 31 日前必须剥离银行——最可能是 1980 年下半年分拆给股东。"1970 年《银行控股公司法》修正案迫使伯克希尔出售伊利诺伊银行：因为伯克希尔同时控股保险、纺织等非银行业务，不能再同时控制银行。这是监管对伯克希尔结构的一次强制干预。

讽刺的是：这家银行是组合中资产收益率最高的资产之一，却因法律必须放手。这也解释了为何伯克希尔此后对银行只做少数股权（如 1990 年 Wells Fargo）——不控股就不用被迫剥离。1980 年信中巴菲特详细讨论了分拆方案与股东税负安排。 
Retailing
Upon merging with Diversified, we acquired 100% ownership of
Associated Retail Stores, Inc., a chain of about 75 popular
priced womens apparel stores.  Associated was launched in
Chicago on March 7, 1931 with one store, $3200, and two
extraordinary partners, Ben Rosner and Leo Simon.  After Mr.
Simons death, the business was offered to Diversified for cash
in 1967.  Ben was to continue running the business - and run it,
he has.
"与 Diversified 合并后，我们获得 Associated Retail Stores 100% 股权——约 75 家平价女装店，1931 年以 $3,200 起家。"Ben Rosner 与 Leo Simon 两个合伙人 1931 年在芝加哥开出第一家店；Simon 去世后，1967 年整店卖给 Diversified，Ben 继续经营至今。巴菲特用"run it, he has"（他确实在经营）表达对 Ben 的最高敬意。

$3,200 起步的生意穿越近 50 年——巴菲特偏爱这种"小生意+大人物"的组合：生意可以平凡，经营者必须非凡。

Associateds business has not grown, and it consistently has faced adverse demographic and retailing trends. But Bens combination of merchandising, real estate and cost-containment skills has produced an outstanding record of profitability, with returns on capital necessarily employed in the business often in the 20% after-tax area.

"Associated 规模没增长、逆着人口与零售趋势——但 Ben 用商品、地产与成本控制三件套做出常年后税 20% 的资本回报。"这是"夕阳行业中的优秀经营者"范本：行业逆风（人口外流、零售结构变化）挡不住顶级管理者的效率。

与纺织形成对照：同样逆风，纺织是"好管理也难有好回报"（重资产），零售是"好管理能创造高回报"（轻资产、周转快）。巴菲特用这两个案例演示"行业结构 vs 管理层"的相对权重——行业决定天花板，管理层决定能不能摸到。

Ben is now 75 and, like Gene Abegg, 81, at Illinois National and Louie Vincenti, 73, at Wesco, continues daily to bring an almost passionately proprietary attitude to the business. This group of top managers must appear to an outsider to be an overreaction on our part to an OEO bulletin on age discrimination. While unorthodox, these relationships have been exceptionally rewarding, both financially and personally. It is a real pleasure to work with managers who enjoy coming to work each morning and, once there, instinctively and unerringly think like owners. We are associated with some of the very best.

"Ben 75 岁、Gene 81 岁、Louie 73 岁——一群像老板一样思考的经理人。"巴菲特自嘲：这帮高龄经理人"看起来像是对 OEO 反年龄歧视通告的过度反应"。他强调与"享受每天上班、本能地像所有者一样思考"的人共事，是财务与个人的双重回报。

高龄+激情=巴菲特最爱的组合：年龄意味着经验与长期磨合，激情意味着不用监督。1970 年代市场共识是"管理层越年轻越好"，巴菲特反其道而行——他买的是"所有者心态"，与年龄无关。

Warren E. Buffett, Chairman

March 26, 1979

Berkshire Hathaway Letter — 1980

Non-Controlled Ownership Earnings — The Iceberg

Many of these companies pay out relatively small proportions of their earnings in dividends. This means that only a small proportion of their current earning power is recorded in our own current operating earnings. But, while our reported operating earnings reflect only the dividends received from such companies, our economic well-being is determined by their earnings, not their dividends... the part of "our" earnings that these companies retained last year (the part not paid to us in dividends) exceeded the total reported annual operating earnings of Berkshire Hathaway. Thus, conventional accounting only allows less than half of our earnings "iceberg" to appear above the surface, in plain view.

"冰山"——报表利润只是水面上的部分。1980 年巴菲特提出了一个震撼性的概念：Berkshire 持有的非控股公司（<20% 持股）保留的利润（不分配给 Berkshire 的部分）已经超过了 Berkshire 自己报表上的全部经营利润。也就是说，Berkshire 的真实盈利能力是报表数字的两倍以上——但 GAAP 只允许记录分红，不记录留存利润。

这个概念后来被称为"look-through earnings"（穿透收益）。巴菲特要股东看的不是 GAAP 利润，而是"我们持有的所有企业（不管控股与否）的利润中属于我们的部分"。1980 年 Berkshire 报表利润 $4190 万，但穿透收益可能在 $8000 万以上。差距来自 GEICO（$2000 万 vs $300 万分红）、华盛顿邮报、SAFECO 等高留存利润公司。这些公司不分配利润不是因为不需要钱，而是因为留存利润的再投资回报率高于分红给股东的税后回报——对 Berkshire 来说，让好公司留存利润比收到分红更好。

The value to Berkshire Hathaway of retained earnings is not determined by whether we own 100%, 50%, 20% or 1% of the businesses in which they reside. Rather, the value of those retained earnings is determined by the use to which they are put and the subsequent level of earnings produced by that usage... If a tree grows in a forest partially owned by us, but we don't record the growth in our financial statements, we still own part of the tree.

"树在森林里长，不管你记不记录，你拥有它的一部分。"这是对 GAAP 会计最优雅的批判。留存利润的价值不取决于你拥有多少股份，而取决于管理层如何使用它。100% 控股一个差公司（如纺织），留存利润的回报率低于市场——100% 的"控制"只是理论上的，因为不投入就无法维持资产价值。相反，1% 持股一个好公司（如 GEICO），留存利润的回报率远超市场——1% 的"非控制"反而创造了更大价值。

"是行为重要，不是行为者。"——关键不在于谁做决策（你选的经理人 vs 你自己），而在于决策的质量。巴菲特宁愿让 GEICO 的 John Byrne 管理留存利润，也不愿自己管——因为 Byrne 在保险业的再投资能力远强于巴菲特。这就是"不控股比控股更好"的底层逻辑：好公司的管理层比你更擅长配置资本。

One usage of retained earnings we often greet with special enthusiasm when practiced by companies in which we have an investment interest is repurchase of their own shares. The reasoning is simple: if a fine business is selling in the market place for far less than intrinsic value, what more certain or more profitable utilization of capital can there be than significant enlargement of the interests of all owners at that bargain price? The competitive nature of corporate acquisition activity almost guarantees the payment of a full - frequently more than full - price when a company buys the entire ownership of another enterprise. But the auction nature of security markets often allows finely-run companies the opportunity to purchase portions of their own businesses at a price under 50% of that needed to acquire the same earning power through acquisition.

"回购是留存利润最好的用途之一。"逻辑极简：好公司股价低于内在价值时，回购 = 以 5 折买自己的生意。对比收购：收购要付全价甚至溢价（竞争性竞价），回购可以在公开市场以折扣价买入。同样的盈利能力，回购成本只有收购的一半以下。

但巴菲特强调前提条件：(1) 好公司（有持续盈利能力）；(2) 股价低于内在价值。如果差公司回购（用借来的钱买高估的股票），那是毁灭价值。后来巴菲特在 2011 年正式确立了 Berkshire 的回购政策：当股价低于账面价值的 120% 时回购。2018 年改为"低于内在价值时回购"——更灵活但也更依赖巴菲特的判断。Berkshire 自己在 2020-2024 年回购了约 $700 亿股票——这是对"回购是最好的资本用途"理念的最好实践。

GEICO Corp. — The Best of All Investment Worlds

Our largest non-controlled holding is 7.2 million shares of GEICO Corp., equal to about a 33% equity interest... At the present dividend rate, our reported earnings from GEICO amount to a little over $3 million annually. But we estimate our share of its earning power is on the order of $20 million annually. Thus, undistributed earnings applicable to this holding alone may amount to 40% of total reported operating earnings of Berkshire.

GEICO represents the best of all investment worlds - the coupling of a very important and very hard to duplicate business advantage with an extraordinary management whose skills in operations are matched by skills in capital allocation.

GEICO：$4700 万投入，年盈利能力 $2000 万——ROE 超过 40%。1980 年 GEICO 持仓成本 $4713 万，Berkshire 权益的年盈利能力约 $2000 万——意味着市盈率不到 2.4 倍。这是巴菲特最划算的投资之一。但报表上只显示 $300 万分红——$1700 万的"隐藏利润"不在财报里。

"所有投资世界中最好的"——巴菲特用最高赞誉形容 GEICO：(1) 不可复制的商业优势（直销模式→低成本→低费率→好客户→低理赔→更低费率的飞轮）；(2) 卓越的管理层（John Byrne 同时擅长运营和资本配置）。Byrne 在 1976 年接手濒临破产的 GEICO，4 年内恢复盈利并重建资本。巴菲特后来在 1995 年全资收购 GEICO——1980 年的 $4700 万投资在 15 年后变成了数十亿。GEICO 至今仍是 Berkshire 保险帝国的核心——2024 年贡献约 $40 亿承保利润。

Inflation and Retained Earnings

For capital to be truly indexed, return on equity must rise, i.e., business earnings consistently must increase in proportion to the increase in the price level without any need for the business to add to capital - including working capital - employed. (Increased earnings produced by increased investment don't count.) Only a few businesses come close to exhibiting this ability. And Berkshire Hathaway isn't one of them.

"真正的抗通胀需要 ROE 随通胀自动上升——且不需要额外资本。"这是对"留存利润能抗通胀"观点的反驳。很多人以为"留存利润=自动增长=抗通胀"——巴菲特说不对。如果留存利润只是被重新投入现有业务，而 ROE 不变，那实际回报率被通胀侵蚀。真正的抗通胀企业需要：(1) 不需要额外资本就能提高盈利（定价权）；(2) ROE 随通胀上升。

满足这两个条件的企业极少——巴菲特说 Berkshire 自己都不是。See's Candies 接近（品牌定价权+轻资产），但大部分企业做不到。这意味着在高通胀环境下，即使 Berkshire 年化 20%，股东的实际购买力也可能不增长——这是 1979 年"通胀税"论点的延续。1980 年美国通胀 13.5%——是二战后最高。直到 1982 年 Volcker 加息把通胀压到 4% 以下，这个焦虑才缓解。

In Memory of Gene Abegg

Gene Abegg, founder of our long-owned bank in Rockford, died on July 2, 1980 at the age of 82... From the time we first met, Gene shot straight 100% of the time - the only behavior pattern he had within him. At the outset of negotiations, he laid all negative factors face up on the table; on the other hand, for years after the transaction was completed he would tell me periodically of some previously undiscussed items of value that had come with our purchase... He never delayed reporting a problem for a minute, but problems were few with Gene. What else would you expect from a man who, at the time of the bank holiday in 1933, had enough cash on the premises to pay all depositors in full? Gene never forgot he was handling other people's money.

"Gene 从不忘记他在处理别人的钱。"1933 年银行假日（Bank Holiday）时，Abegg 的银行有足够现金全额支付所有储户——在大部分银行关门的恐慌中，这是极端的保守和负责。巴菲特用 Abegg 的生平来定义 Berkshire 的fiduciary文化：永远把储户/股东的钱当自己的钱来管。

"卖掉公司后比当老板时还努力"——Abegg 71 岁卖掉银行后继续为 Berkshire 工作，比为自己干还卖力。这种"卖后继续拼命"的模式在 Berkshire 的收购中反复出现：Ringwalt、Raab、B夫人、Chuck Huggins——全部如此。为什么？因为巴菲特给他们的不是"雇佣合同"，而是"信任契约"——你可以像以前一样经营，我只是提供资本和后盾。对创始人型管理者来说，这比被大公司"整合"有吸引力得多。Abegg 的银行在 1980 年被剥离出 Berkshire，但他的文化基因永远留在了 Berkshire——"处理别人的钱"的fiduciary精神。

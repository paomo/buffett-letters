Buffett Partnership Letter — 1957

沃伦·巴菲特 · 27岁 · 合伙基金管理规模约 $303,726

The general stock market in 1957 declined from the level reached at the end of 1956. The Dow-Jones Industrial Average declined from 499 to 435, a drop of 64 points. The average would have declined somewhat more than this without the dividend income which was received during the year. If dividends had been reinvested, the overall decline would have been approximately 8.4%.

1957年是熊市。道指从499跌到435，跌幅约13%（含分红回投后约8.4%）。这是战后牛市的第一次显著回调——1956年下半年市场已开始走弱，1957年8月更是单月暴跌（"艾森豪威尔衰退"期间，美联储紧缩+苏伊士危机余波）。巴菲特写这封信时只有27岁，管理着约30万美元的合伙基金。注意他首先报告市场环境，而非自己的业绩——这成为他一生信件的固定结构：先说大盘，再说自己。

The securities in the portfolio during the past year have been confined to situations which I considered undervalued. This policy has resulted in the portfolio being ratherinactive during a declining market. I make no attempt to forecast the general market. My efforts are devoted toward finding undervalued securities. However, I do believe that the widespread attitude that the stock market is a "sure thing" and that all one has to do is to ride along will probably end up causing trouble.

"我无意预测市场，只专注于寻找低估的证券。"这段话定调了巴菲特一生的方法论。1957年牛市刚过，散户普遍认为股市是"稳赚不赔"的，巴菲特却在此刻明确警示这种态度"最终会惹麻烦"。他的组合在下跌市场中"相当不活跃"——因为他持有的低估股不愿在低价卖出。这种耐心和纪律，在27岁时已经成型。

All of the general partners participated in the form of performance allotments as provided in the original partnership agreement. This participation was based on capital at the beginning of the year. The limited partners received 6% on their capital during the year. The general partner's participation after the 6% payment to limited partners amounted to $1,408.50, making the total net worth of the partnerships $404,433.13 at the end of the year.

利润分配机制。合伙基金的结构是：有限合伙人先拿6%的保底收益，超出部分巴菲特拿25%提成，亏损则从未来利润中弥补。这个"6-and-25"结构后来成为对冲基金行业的标准模板之一。1957年基金总资产约40万美元，巴菲特个人提成仅$1,408——说明起步阶段规模极小。但费率结构已体现了"利益绑定"的核心理念：他只在合伙人赚到钱时才赚钱。

The results of the past two years have been rather abnormal in that overall stock prices declined during this period. Therefore, the performance of the partnerships has been better than would normally be the case. If stock prices had advanced during this period, I believe the partnerships would have shown a smaller percentage gain than the stock market. We expect to show a better-than-average performance in declining or static markets, but a lesser-than-average performance in advancing markets.

"熊市少亏，牛市跟上。"这是巴菲特对自己策略最坦诚的期望设定。1956-1957连续两年市场下跌，巴菲特的组合反而跑赢了——因为低估股抗跌。但他清醒地认识到：如果是大牛市，自己可能跑不赢指数。这种自我认知极度稀缺。大部分基金经理吹嘘"全天候跑赢"，巴菲特27岁就承认自己的策略有"不适合的环境"。这个思维贯穿他整个职业生涯——1969年解散基金时也是因为"找不到便宜货"。

Although the Dow-Jones Average is a useful measure of stock market performance, the securities which comprise this average are, generally speaking, large companies. This is also true of most investment company portfolios. The securities which are in the partnerships' portfolios are generally of a smaller size. I believe this should result in better-than-average performance during declining markets, but may be a disadvantage in strongly advancing markets.

道指不代表全市场。巴菲特指出他的组合以中小盘为主，而道指是大盘股指数。1957年小盘股跌幅远大于道指成分股，但巴菲特的低估选股反而提供了下行保护。这段话隐含一个重要观点：基准指数可能高估或低估真实市场广度。60年后，他在伯克希尔信中仍反复强调"不要用单一指数衡量市场"。另外，"小盘低估"策略在此后十年为合伙基金贡献了惊人回报——直到1969年规模太大、小盘已无便宜货可买时才终止。

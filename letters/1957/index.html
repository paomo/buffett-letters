Buffett Partnership Letter — 1957

沃伦·巴菲特 · 27岁 · 合伙基金约 $570,000 · 奥马哈

时代背景 · 1957

宏观CPI通胀3.3%，经济温和衰退（1957.8-1958.4），失业率从4.2%升至6.3%
市场道指从499跌至435（-8.5%），战后第一次中等幅度回调
政治艾森豪威尔第二任期开始，9月小石城事件（种族隔离危机），10月苏联发射Sputnik卫星（太空竞赛开始）
Berkshire合伙基金成立第二年，管理约$57万，三个合伙基金全部正收益

The General Stock Market Picture in 1957

In last year's letter to partners, I said the following: My view of the general market level is that it is priced above intrinsic value. This view relates to blue-chip securities. This view, if accurate, carries with it the possibility of a substantial decline in all stock prices, both undervalued and otherwise. In any event I think the probability is very slight that current market levels will be thought of as cheap five years from now. Even a full-scale bear market, however, should not hurt the market value of our work-outs substantially.

开篇引用上一年信件——建立连续性。1956 年的信（第一封）已警告市场高估，1957 年市场确实下跌，验证了这一判断。但巴菲特没有幸灾乐祸，而是说"五年后回看今天，不太可能觉得便宜"——即使跌了，还是不便宜。这种不因短期正确而膨胀的态度，27 岁已定型。同时首次提到"work-outs"（套利/事件驱动），说熊市也伤不到这类资产——这是他后来"套利+低估"双引擎策略的雏形。

The past year witnessed a moderate decline in stock prices. I stress the word "moderate" since casual reading of the press or conversing with those who have had only recent experience with stocks would tend to create an impression of a much greater decline. Actually, it appears to me that the decline in stock prices has been considerably less than the decline in corporate earning power under present business conditions. This means that the public is still very bullish on blue chip stocks and the general economic picture. I make no attempt to forecast either business or the stock market; the above is simply intended to dispel any notions that stocks have suffered any drastic decline or that the general market, is at a low level. I still consider the general market to be priced on the high side based on long term investment value.

用"moderate"对抗叙事偏差。媒体渲染大跌，但巴菲特用数据说话：股价跌幅小于盈利跌幅，意味着估值反而上升了。公众"仍然非常看好蓝筹"——1957 年的下跌并没有清洗掉投机情绪。这段话的核心洞察是：跌幅大不等于便宜了，要看跌幅是否超过了基本面恶化。这个思维方式在 1973-74、2000-02、2008 年他都在重复。巴菲特一生都在对抗"跌了=便宜了"的线性思维。

Our Activities in 1957

The market decline has created greater opportunity among undervalued situations so that, generally, our portfolio is heavier in undervalued situations relative to work-outs than it was last year. Perhaps an explanation of the term "work-out" is in order. A work-out is an investment which is dependent on a specific corporate action for its profit rather than a general advance in the price of the stock as in the case of undervalued situations. Work-outs come about through: sales, mergers, liquidations, tenders, etc. In each case, the risk is that something will upset the applecart and cause the abandonment of the planned action, not that the economic picture will deteriorate and stocks decline generally. At the end of 1956, we had a ratio of about 70-30 between general issues and work-outs. Now it is about 85-15.

首次系统阐述"双引擎"策略。"低估型"（general issues）依赖股价向价值回归，"套利型"（work-outs）依赖并购/清算等公司事件。两者相关性低——套利不随大盘涨跌。1957 年熊市让低估机会增多，比例从 70:30 变为 85:15。这个框架后来演变为伯克希尔的"长期持有+套利+现金"三层结构。巴菲特在 1988 年信中回顾套利时仍提到这一分类。

During the past year we have taken positions in two situations which have reached a size where we may expect to take some part in corporate decisions. One of these positions accounts for between 10% and 20% of the portfolio of the various partnerships and the other accounts for about 5%. Both of these will probably take in the neighborhood of three to five years of work but they presently appear to have potential for a high average annual rate of return with a minimum of risk. While not in the classification of work-outs, they have very little dependence on the general action of the stock market.

27 岁的"激进投资者"。巴菲特已在通过大持股介入公司决策——这就是后来 Sanborn Map（1958）、Dempster Mill（1962）、Berkshire（1965）的预演。10-20% 的集中度对于一个 30 万美元的基金来说意味着重仓少数标的。注意他说"三到五年"——这个时间框架和今天伯克希尔持有可口可乐 35 年的逻辑一脉相承：找的是长期价值释放，不是短期价格博弈。

Results for 1957

In 1957 the three partnerships which we formed in 1956 did substantially better than the general market. At the beginning of the year, the Dow-Jones Industrials stood at 499 and at the end of the year it was at 435 for a loss of 64 points. If one had owned the Averages, he would have received 22 points in dividends reducing the overall loss to 42 points or 8.470% for the year. This loss is roughly equivalent to what would have been achieved by investing in most investment funds and, to my knowledge, no investment fund invested in stocks showed a gain for the year.

道指 -8.5%，三个合伙基金全部正收益。回报分别为 6.2%、7.8%、25%。差异巨大——第三个基金晚成立，恰逢低点，"抄到了底"。巴菲特对此极其坦诚：他说是"运气"。多数基金经理会拿 25% 那个数字大做文章，巴菲特却逐个列出并解释差异来源。这种透明度在 1957 年的资管行业极为罕见。

Naturally a question is created as to the vastly superior performance of the last partnership, particularly in the mind of the partners of the first two. This performance emphasizes the importance of luck in the short run, particularly in regard to when funds are received. The third partnership was started the latest in 1956 when the market was at a lower level and when several securities were particularly attractive. Because of the availability of funds, large positions were taken in these issues. Whereas the two partnerships formed earlier were already substantially invested so that they could only take relatively small positions in these issues.

坦承运气的作用。"资金的到达时间"决定短期业绩差异——这是对 LP 最诚实的交代。大部分基金经理把好业绩归因于能力，差业绩归因于市场。巴菲特反着来：好业绩先说运气。这种态度为他赢得了长期信任——1969 年解散基金时，没有合伙人抱怨，因为他们从头到尾都知道自己在买什么。

Over the years, I will be quite satisfied with a performance that is 10% per year better than the Averages, so in respect to these three partnerships, 1957 was a successful and probably better than average, year.

定下目标：年化跑赢道指 10 个百分点。这是巴菲特整个合伙基金时期（1957-1969）的基准。实际成绩：13 年年化约 29.5%，道指约 7.4%，超额约 22%——远超 10% 目标。但注意他用"quite satisfied"而非"target"——他不想让 LP 把 10% 当承诺。这是管理预期，不是设定承诺。

Interpretation of results

To some extent our better than average performance in 1957 was due to the fact that it was a generally poor year for most stocks. Our performance, relatively, is likely to be better in a bear market than in a bull market so that deductions made from the above results should be tempered by the fact that it was the type of year when we should have done relatively well. In a year when the general market had a substantial advance I would be well satisfied to match the advance of the Averages.

最重要的预期管理段落。"熊市跑赢，牛市跟上"——这个框架在 1958 年立即兑现：牛市来了，巴菲特基本持平道指。他提前打了预防针。这不是谦虚，是策略特征：低估股在牛市中天然落后，因为市场追逐的是成长和故事，不是价值。LP 如果不理解这一点，在牛市中就会赎回——巴菲特用这段话提前锁定了理解。

I can definitely say that our portfolio represents better value at the end of 1957 than it did at the end of 1956. This is due to both generally lower prices and the fact that we have had more time to acquire the more substantially undervalued securities which can only be acquired with patience. Earlier I mentioned our largest position which comprised 10% to 20% of the assets of the various partnerships. In time I plan to have this represent 20% of the assets of all partnerships but this cannot be hurried. Obviously during any acquisition period, our primary interest is to have the stock do nothing or decline rather than advance. Therefore, at any given time, a fair proportion of our portfolio may be in the sterile stage. This policy, while requiring patience, should maximize long term profits.

"我希望我的股票不涨或下跌。"这是整封信最反直觉的一句话。在建仓期，股价不动或下跌才是最优——因为能以更低成本积累。巴菲特称之为"sterile stage"（休眠期）。大部分投资者无法忍受持仓长期不涨，但巴菲特把这当作买入机会的延续。这种耐心在 1958 年的 Commonwealth Trust 案例中得到了完美体现：他花了一年多慢慢买入一只几乎无人交易的银行股。耐心不是美德，是策略。

年度核心

  • 双引擎策略初现（低估+套利）
  • 熊市跑赢，牛市跟上--预期管理框架
  • 定下目标：年化跑赢道指10%

1974 Letter

To the Stockholders of Berkshire Hathaway Inc.:

Operating results for 1974 overall were unsatisfactory due to the poor performance of our insurance business. In last year’s annual report some decline in profitability was predicted but the extent of this decline, which accelerated during the year, was a surprise. Operating earnings for 1974 were $8,383,576, or $8.56 per share, for a return on beginning shareholders’ equity of 10.3%. This is the lowest return on equity realized since 1970. Our textile division and our bank both performed very well, turning in improved results against the already good figures of 1973.

ROE 10.3%——自 1970 年以来最低。1974 年是 1929 年以来美股最惨的一年：道指从 851 跌到 616（-28%），标普 500 PE 跌到 7 倍。Berkshire 的纺织和银行业务表现不错，但保险业务急剧恶化——之前几年"不可持续的高利润"终于引来了"不聪明的竞争"，费率被大幅压低。

但 1974 年的真正故事不在年报数字里——而在巴菲特这年做的事情。他在熊市谷底大举加仓华盛顿邮报（从 $17 继续买）、GEICO 优先股、其他低估股。巴菲特后来在 1979 年信中回忆："1974 年是我投资生涯中最兴奋的时期之一——不是因为赚了钱，而是因为到处都是便宜货。"1974 年的浮亏是暂时的，1975-1976 年这些持仓大幅反弹。

However, insurance underwriting, which has been mentioned in the last several annual reports as running at levels of unsustainable profitability, turned dramatically worse as the year progressed.

“承保业务的戏剧性恶化是1974年业绩不达预期的总根源”巴菲特在前几年年报中反复警告：承保利润高到“不可持续”，必然招来不理性的竞争和不足的费率。1974年这些预警同时兑现：通胀每月推高赔付成本约1%，而费率数年未动，利润率的崩坏只是算术题。当时的市场共识是“保险是稳健的收息生意”，巴菲特看到的却是费率—成本剪刀差下的定时炸弹——这正是他后来反复强调的“在别人贪婪时恐惧”。

值得注意的是他把“纺织和银行都很好”与“保险拖累全局”并列陈述：好生意与坏生意在同一家公司并存，管理层的工作不是粉饰，而是如实拆解——这也是1977年“分部报告”（segment-reporting）概念的最早雏形。

The outlook for 1975 is not encouraging. We undoubtedly will have sharply negative comparisons in our textile operation and probably a moderate decline in banking earnings.

1974-75 是巴菲特职业生涯最黑暗的时期之一。但他选择"继续积累财务实力和流动性，为保险费率恢复合理、可以再次激进扩张的时刻做准备"。——反周期准备的宣言：别人恐惧时，正是备战之时。

Insurance underwriting is a large question mark at this time—it certainly won’t be a satisfactory year in this area, and could be an extremely poor one. Prospects are reasonably good for an improvement in both insurance investment income and our equity in earnings of Blue Chip

“巴菲特罕见地把能确定的与不能确定的分开列账”承保是“大问号”，甚至可能“极差”；而保险投资收入和Blue Chip权益收益的改善则是“相当有把握”。在熊市谷底，他没有用模糊的乐观掩盖不确定性，而是精确区分确定与不确定——这份诚实比任何预测都有价值。当时的市场共识是“通胀下一切资产都该抛售”，巴菲特却已经开始为费率修复后的扩张储备弹药。

风险面：1975年承保果真继续恶化（行业综合成本率普遍升至105%以上），但Berkshire因提前收缩承保量而受伤较轻——承认不确定性并据此行动，正是“安全边际”在经营层面的体现。

Stamps. During this period we plan to continue to build financial strength and liquidity, preparing for the time when insurance rates become adequate and we can once again aggressively pursue opportunities for growth in this area.

“继续积累财务实力和流动性，为费率足够、可以再次激进扩张的那一天做准备”——这是1974年最反周期的一段话：行业哀鸿遍野，Berkshire却在备战。巴菲特把熊市当作进货期而非逃生期：保险费率被压到不理性时收缩承保、囤积现金，等对手被红墨水教训够了再出击。这与他在1974年股市谷底逆势加仓华盛顿邮报（从17美元一路买到20美元以上）、加码GEICO是同一逻辑——市场共识是“现金为王、远离股票”，他却认为现金的使命是等待机会。

纵向看，这段话是1982年“当费率足够时再激进”的预告，也是2008年金融危机中伯克希尔以优先股驰援高盛、GE的前身——别人恐惧时的流动性，是最稀缺的资产。

Textile Operations

During the first nine months of 1974 textile demand was exceptionally strong, resulting in very firm prices. However, in the fourth quarter significant weaknesses began to appear, which have continued into 1975.

纺织业前三季度需求强，四季度急转直下——1975 年继续恶化。开工率降到三分之一，必然亏损。窗帘是"可推迟购买清单上的优先项"——经济衰退时消费者先砍这类开支。

We currently are operating at about one‐third of capacity. Obviously, at such levels operating losses must result. As shipments have fallen, we continuously have adjusted our level of operations downward so as to avoid building inventory.

“开工率降到三分之一，亏损是算术必然——但巴菲特选择主动减产、不囤库存”纺织是典型的资本密集同质化行业：需求萎缩时增产只会让价格崩得更快。他的应对不是死扛产能，而是随订单下滑不断调低开工率、避免库存堆积——把“卖不掉的存货”这个更深的陷阱提前避开。当时的市场共识是衰退期应“薄利多销抢现金流”，巴菲特却宁可让产能闲置也不污染价格。

这段是纺织业务价值毁灭的实况记录：1974年它还在贡献利润，1985年终于被关停——从三分之一开工率到彻底退出，巴菲特用十年验证了“在错误的行业里，最好的管理也只能延缓亏损”。

Our products are largely in the curtain goods area. During a period of consumer uncertainty, curtains may well be high on the list of deferrable purchases. Very low levels of housing starts also serve to dampen demand. In addition, retailers have been pressing to cut inventories generally, and we probably are feeling some effect from these efforts. These negative trends should reverse in due course, and we are attempting to minimize losses until that time comes.

“窗帘是可推迟购买清单的榜首——巴菲特对消费品需求弹性的判断精准而冷酷”经济不确定时，消费者最先砍掉的是非紧急软性支出；叠加极低的新屋开工（从1972年约230万套的高峰跌至1975年约116万套）和零售商普遍去库存，三重逆风同时压来。他没有幻想需求马上反转，只说“这些负面趋势迟早会逆转，在那之前把亏损压到最小”。

这与1972年买入See’s糖果形成对照：同样是消费品，糖果在衰退中逆势增长（1974年See’s表现优异），窗帘却首当其冲——需求的“可推迟性”决定了生意的抗周期能力。选择大于努力，这是他后来反复强调的。

Insurance Underwriting

In the last few years we consistently have commented on the unusual profitability in insurance underwriting. This seemed certain eventually to attract unintelligent competition with consequent inadequate rates. It also has been apparent that many insurance organizations, major as well as minor, have been guilty of significant underreserving of losses, which inevitably produces faulty information as to the true cost of the product being sold. In 1974, these factors, along with a high rate of inflation, combined to produce a rapid erosion in underwriting results.

保险承保恶化的根源分析。巴菲特早已警告：不寻常的高利润会引来"不聪明的竞争"+"准备不足"（损失准备金不足）。1974 年这些因素叠加高通胀，快速侵蚀承保结果。产品成本（修车、医疗、赔偿）每月涨约 1%，而费率多年未变——"预测利润率的走向并不难"。

The costs of the product we deliver (auto repair, medical payments, compensation benefits, etc.) are increasing at a rate we estimate to be in the area of 1% per month. Of course, this increase doesn’t proceed in an even flow but, inexorably, inflation grinds very heavily at the repair services—to humans and to property—that we provide. However, rates virtually have been unchanged in the property and casualty field for the last few years. With costs moving forward rapidly and prices remaining unchanged, it was not hard to predict what would happen to profit margins.

“成本以每月约1%的速度上涨（年化约12%），而费率数年未动——利润率的崩坏不需要预测，只需要算术”巴菲特拆解了承保亏损的微观机制：修车、医疗、工伤赔偿这些产品成本的原材料在通胀下月月涨价，而财产险费率却几乎冻结。保费是“销售额”，赔款和费用是“销售成本”，两者剪刀差一旦拉开，利润率转负只是时间问题。

当时的市场共识把保险股当“通胀对冲”买，巴菲特却指出：在费率管制下，保险恰恰是通胀的最大受害者之一——成本可以即时上涨，价格却要等监管批准。这也是他后来反复强调“保险是浮存金生意、承保必须盈利”的起点：浮存金的成本是负还是正，取决于承保纪律。

Best’s, the authoritative voice of the insurance industry, estimates that in 1974 all auto insurance premiums in the United States increased only about 2%. Such a growth in the pool of dollars available to pay insured losses and expenses was woefully inadequate. Obviously, medical costs applicable to people injured during the year, jury awards for pain and suffering, and body shop charges for repairing damaged cars increased at a dramatically greater rate during the year. Since premiums represent the sales dollar and the latter items represent the cost of goods sold, profit margins turned sharply negative.

数据证明承保危机。1974 年全美车险保费只涨 2%，而医疗成本、陪审团判决、修车费涨幅远超——"保费是销售额，赔款是销售成本，利润率必然转负"。巴菲特引用 Best's 数据，说明这不是他一家的问题，是全行业的系统性危机。

As this report is being written, such deterioration continues. Loss reserves for many giant companies still appear to be understated by significant amounts, which means that these competitors continue to underestimate their true costs. Not only must rates be increased sufficiently to match the month‐by‐month increase in cost levels, but the existed expense‐ revenue gap must be overcome. At this time it appears that insurors must experience even more devastating underwriting results before they take appropriate pricing action.

恶化仍在继续：大公司的损失准备金仍被低估——"竞争者继续低估真实成本"。要扭转局面，保险公司必须先承受"更具破坏性的承保结果"才会采取正确的定价行动。这是周期的残酷现实。

All major areas of insurance operations, except for the “home state” companies, experienced significantly poorer results for the year.

“全线溃败中唯一的例外——本州公司”这句承上启下：全行业承保恶化，唯独Home State模式（直接面向车主、无中间代理人）保持了可接受的损失率。在别人亏损的地方赚钱，靠的不是运气，而是模式差异——本州公司绕开代理人佣金、贴近本地定价，费率纪律更容易执行。巴菲特特意在悲观章节里点出这处亮色，说明他看业务从来不看行业平均，而看“具体模式在具体市场里能否赚钱”。

The direct business of National Indemnity Company, our largest area of insurance activity, produced an underwriting loss of approximately 4% after several years of high profitability.

National Indemnity 承保亏损约 4%（此前多年高盈利）。巴菲特不鼓励业务量增长——"直到费率更合理"。历史规律：大公司被"红墨水"教训后，会有业务以合理费率流入。"由 Phil Liesche 领导，充满利润导向的人。"——等待周期反转。

Volume increased somewhat, but we are not encouraging such increases until rates are more adequate. At some point in the cycle, after major insurance companies have had their fill of red ink, history indicates that we will experience an inflow of business at compensatory rates. This operation, headed by Phil Liesche, a most able underwriter, is staffed by highly profit‐oriented people and we believe it will provide excellent earnings in most future years, as it has in the

“等到大保险公司被红墨水灌饱，历史表明会有业务以补偿性费率流回来”——这是巴菲特对保险周期最凝练的概括：价格战总有终点，纪律差的公司先退出，幸存者才能在费率修复期接到“送上门”的业务。所以他拒绝在费率不足时追量（量增了，亏损也增了），宁可让National Indemnity缩量等待。

纵向看，这是逆势收缩的经典案例：多数管理层在衰退期靠降价保份额，巴菲特却用短期规模换长期定价权——与1973年他退出部分再保险合约、1980年代拒绝垃圾费率是同一纪律。风险面：等待可能很长，但他用“绝不补贴亏损业务”的底线对冲了时间成本。

Intense competition in the reinsurance business has produced major losses for practically every company operating in the area. We have been no exception. Our underwriting loss was something over 12%—a horrendous figure, but probably little different from the average of the industry. What is even more frightening is that, while about the usual number of insurance catastrophes occurred during 1974, there really was no “super disaster” which might have accounted for the poor figures of the industry. Rather, a condition of inadequate rates prevails, particularly in the casualty area where we have significant exposure. Our reinsurance department is run by George Young, an exceptionally competent and hardworking manager. He has cancelled a great many contracts where prices are totally inadequate, and is making no attempt to increase volume except in areas where premiums are commensurate with risk.

再保险亏损超 12%——"骇人听闻"。更可怕的是：1974 年没有"超级灾难"，行业却普遍巨亏——纯粹是费率不足。George Young 取消了大量费率完全不足的合同，"除了保费与风险相称的领域，不试图增加量"。——宁缺毋滥。

Based upon present rate levels, it seems highly unlikely that the reinsurance industry generally, or we, specifically, will have a profitable year in 1975.

“把丑话说在前面：按现行费率，1975年再保险几乎不可能盈利，我们也不例外”这不是谦虚，是精确的行业判断——1974年没有超级灾难、行业却巨亏12%以上，说明问题不是运气而是费率结构。巴菲特不回避自家亏损，但强调George Young已经砍掉了大量费率完全不足的合同、“只在保费与风险相称的地方增量”——亏损是主动选择，不是被动挨打。

这与Florida的教训互为镜像：在能算清风险的地方果断收缩（再保险），在算不清风险的地方坚决退出（Florida）——“不懂不做”与“懂了就做”是同一枚硬币。

Our “home state” companies, under the leadership of John Ringwalt, made good progress in 1974. We appear to be developing a sound agency group, capable of producing business with acceptable loss ratios. Our expense ratios still are much too high, but will come down as the operation develops into units of economic size. The Texas problem which was commented upon in last year’s report seems to be improving. We consider the “home state” operation one of our most promising areas for the future.

“本州公司是1974年保险板块唯一的增长点”John Ringwalt带队构建的代理人体系开始成型，损失率可接受；费用率仍偏高，但会随规模经济摊薄；去年年报提到的德州问题也在改善。巴菲特称之为“未来最有前景的领域之一”——在别人亏损的市场里，一个能做出可接受损失率的细分模式，就是下一轮周期的种子。

对照Florida的溃败：同样是扩张，Home State是“在熟悉的市场用验证过的模式慢慢长”，Florida是“在不熟悉的市场用不完整的定价数据快进”——前者亏在速度上，后者败在无知上。能力圈不是口号，是具体的定价知识。

Our efforts to expand Home and Automobile Insurance Company into Florida proved disastrous. The underwriting loss from operations in that market will come to over $2 million, a very large portion of which was realized in 1974. We made the decision to drop out of the Florida market in the middle of 1974, but losses in substantial amounts have continued since that time because of the term nature of insurance contracts, as well as adverse development of outstanding claims. We can’t blame external insurance industry conditions for this mistake. In retrospect, it is apparent that our management simply did not have the underwriting information and the pricing knowledge necessary to be operating in the area. In Cook County, where Home and Auto’s volume traditionally has been concentrated, evidence also became quite clear during 1974 that rates were inadequate. Therefore, rates were increased during the middle of the year but competition did not follow; consequently, our volume has dropped significantly in this area as competitors take business from us at prices that we regard as totally unrealistic.

Florida 扩张失败——亏损超 $200 万。巴菲特罕见地承认错误："我们不能责怪外部行业环境。事后看来，我们的管理层根本没有承保信息和定价知识来做这个市场。"1974 年中退出佛罗里达，但保险合同期限+未决索赔让损失继续。这是"不懂不做"原则的反面教材。

While the tone of this section is pessimistic as to 1974 and 1975, we consider the insurance business to be inherently attractive. Our overall return on capital employed in this area—even including the poor results of 1974—remains high. We have made every effort to be realistic in the calculation of loss and administrative expense. Because of accruals, this had a double effect at both the bank and corporate level in 1974.

尽管短期悲观，巴菲特强调："我们认为保险业务本质上具有吸引力。"整体资本回报仍然很高——即使算上 1974 年的糟糕结果。他坚持保守计提损失和费用（"双重影响"了 1974 年银行和企业层面的利润）——宁可现在多提，不为未来埋雷。

Under present money market conditions, we expect bank earnings to be down somewhat in 1975 although we believe they still are likely to compare favorably with those of practically any

“1975年银行盈利预计小幅下滑，但仍可能好于全国几乎任何一家银行”高利率环境（1974年联邦基金利率一度升至12%以上）压缩净息差，巴菲特对银行的预期务实但不悲观。这句话的潜台词是：银行的差异远大于行业的周期——自家伊利诺伊国民银行（Illinois National Bank）在1974年依然表现稳健，靠的是保守的贷款文化和成本纪律，而不是行业beta。

这是巴菲特“选生意不如选管理加文化”的早期版本：同样的货币环境，不同银行的结果天差地别。风险面：他并未预言1975年银行会出问题，但承认盈利会降——把“下滑但优于同行”作为基准，是给股东的合理预期管理。

“熊市谷底，See’s糖果交出出色的一年——消费特许经营权的第一次绽放”1972年以2500万美元买下See’s时，市场共识是“出价太贵”；1974年经济衰退中糖果销售依然强劲，证明优质品牌的抗周期能力。巴菲特后来多次说，See’s让他第一次理解了品牌定价权的价值——好生意在坏年份依然赚钱，坏生意在好年份也赚不了多少。

这是“以合理价格买伟大公司”理念的起点：Berkshire此后所有消费类投资（可口可乐、DQ、喜诗糖果）都在这棵树上嫁接。当时没人能预见到，这家糖果店会成为巴菲特投资哲学的转折点。

“一张拼图：巴菲特同时是Blue Chip的董事、Wesco（持股64%）的董事、See’s的董事长”1974年Berkshire把Blue Chip持股增至25.5%，通过董事会席位深度参与三家公司的经营——这是“不控股的控股”（non-control-advantage）的早期形态：不并表，却能影响决策、分享利润、积累行业认知。Wesco 64%的持股和See’s的董事会席位，让Berkshire在蓝筹印花这家“壳公司”里同时握有银行（Wesco旗下互助储蓄）、糖果（See’s）和印花业务。

“我们预期Blue Chip将成为Berkshire持续而可观的盈利来源”印花销售已大幅萎缩，但巴菲特看的是组合而非单一业务：See’s的现金流、Wesco的银行和保险业务，共同构成Blue Chip的透视盈利（look-through earnings）——这个1980年才系统阐述的概念，1974年已经在实践中使用。市场共识盯着蓝筹印花“过气的商业模式”卖出估值，巴菲特却按旗下资产的盈利能力重新定价。

“合并终止是双方董事会一致同意的结果——没有敌意，只有时机未到”巴菲特特意强调终止不是单方放弃，并预告“希望未来重提”，等于给股东吃定心丸：战略判断没变，变的只是交易结构。当时的市场共识会把合并破裂读成利空信号，巴菲特却把它当作一次定价分歧的搁置——好交易值得等，坏交易值得放弃，判断标准是内在价值而不是消息本身。

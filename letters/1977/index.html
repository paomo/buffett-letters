Berkshire Hathaway Letter — 1977

沃伦·巴菲特 · 47岁 · ROE 19% · 保险保费 $1.51亿 · 股票组合 $1.81亿 · 1978年3月

时代背景 · 1977

宏观GDP增速4.6%，通胀6.5%，通胀回落但仍高
市场道指从974跌至831（-14.7%），年底调整
政治卡特就任，能源危机持续，纽约大停电（7月）
BerkshireROE 19%，保费$1.51亿，股票组合$1.81亿，正式股东信元年

Most companies define "record" earnings as a new high in earnings per share. Since businesses customarily add from year to year to their equity base, we find nothing particularly noteworthy in a management performance combining, say, a 10% increase in equity capital and a 5% increase in earnings per share. After all, even a totally dormant savings account will produce steadily rising interest earnings each year because of compounding... We believe a more appropriate measure of managerial economic performance to be return on equity capital. In 1977 our operating earnings on beginning equity capital amounted to 19%.

"即使一个完全休眠的储蓄账户也会因为复利而产生逐年增长的利息。"巴菲特在此确立了一个核心原则：EPS 增长不等于管理好——因为留存利润自动推高 EPS。如果公司留存了 10% 的利润但 EPS 只涨 5%，实际是缩水了。正确衡量管理表现的是 ROE——1977 年 19%，略高于长期平均。

这个概念后来在 1979 年发展为"留存收益的测试"：每 $1 留存利润必须创造至少 $1 的市场价值。巴菲特在 1983 年正式引入"每股内在价值"概念，作为比账面价值和 EPS 更好的衡量标准。1977 年的信是这些思想的起点——从质疑 EPS 开始，逐步构建了 Berkshire 独特的价值衡量体系。

Textile Operations

A few shareholders have questioned the wisdom of remaining in the textile business which, over the longer term, is unlikely to produce returns on capital comparable to those available in many other businesses. Our reasons are several: (1) Our mills are among the largest employers in each town, utilizing a labor force of high average age possessing relatively non-transferable skills... (2) management also has been energetic and straightforward... Ken Chace's efforts after the change in corporate control took place in 1965 generated capital from the textile division needed to finance the acquisition and expansion of our profitable insurance operation. (3) With hard work and some imagination... at least modest profits in the textile division can be achieved in the future.

保留纺织的三个理由——以及背后的"顺风/逆风"哲学。巴菲特解释为什么不关纺织厂：(1) 社会责任（大龄工人无法转行）；(2) Ken Chace 的努力（1965 年后纺织产生的现金流资助了保险收购）；(3) 未来可能有微利。这三条理由中，只有 (2) 是经济理性——(1) 是感情，(3) 是希望。巴菲特后来承认这些理由不够充分——1985 年关闭纺织时他说"太晚了"。

更重要的是后文的一句话："One of the lessons your management has learned is the importance of being in businesses where tailwinds prevail rather than headwinds."（顺风 vs 逆风）。纺织是逆风行业——不管管理多好都只能微利。保险是顺风行业——即使犯了一些错误（信中列举了四个失败案例），整体仍然大赚。这个"选赛道比选管理更重要"的认知，直接影响了后来 Berkshire 的收购策略：只买有好经济特征（顺风）的企业。

Insurance Underwriting — The Nature of the Business

Insurance companies offer standardized policies which can be copied by anyone. Their only products are promises. It is not difficult to be licensed, and rates are an open book. There are no important advantages from trademarks, patents, location, corporate longevity, raw material sources, etc., and very little consumer differentiation to produce insulation from competition. It is commonplace, in corporate annual reports, to stress the difference that people make. Sometimes this is true and sometimes it isn't. But there is no question that the nature of the insurance business magnifies the effect which individual managers have on company performance.

"保险唯一的产品是承诺。"这是对保险行业最精准的一句话概括。没有品牌护城河（保单标准化）、没有专利壁垒（费率公开）、没有规模优势（小公司也能有效运营）。唯一能区分好坏保险公司的是管理层的承保纪律。在其他行业，平庸的管理者可能靠品牌或专利撑着；在保险行业，平庸的管理者必然亏钱。

"企业文化差异被放大"——因为保险的"产品"是承诺，承诺的好坏取决于谁在做承诺。Phil Liesche 在 National Indemnity 的承保纪律、John Ringwalt 在 homestate 的精耕细作、John Seward 在 Home & Auto 的扭亏为盈——每个人都是"person makes difference"的证明。巴菲特选保险管理人的标准不是聪明，而是纪律——"宁可丢业务也不降价"的纪律。这种纪律在繁荣期看起来愚蠢（丢份额），在萧条期证明是智慧（不亏钱）。

Insurance Investments

Most of our large stock positions are going to be held for many years and the scorecard on our investment decisions will be provided by business results over that period, and not by prices on any given day. Just as it would be foolish to focus unduly on short-term prospects when acquiring an entire company, we think it equally unsound to become mesmerized by prospective near term earnings or recent trends in earnings when purchasing small pieces of a company; i.e., marketable common stocks.

"买股票就是买企业——不管是整个企业还是一小部分。"这是巴菲特投资哲学最核心的一句话。1977 年底组合成本 $1.07 亿，市值 $1.81 亿，浮盈 $7400 万。但巴菲特说"不要看重某一天的数字"——1974 年底浮亏 $1700 万，1977 年底浮盈 $7400 万，波动巨大但无关紧要。

1977 年底前五大持仓：GEICO 优先股（成本 $1940 万，市值 $3300 万）、华盛顿邮报（成本 $1060 万，市值 $3340 万）、Interpublic（成本 $450 万，市值 $1720 万）、Capital Cities（成本 $1090 万，市值 $1320 万）、Kaiser Industries（成本 $80 万，市值 $600 万）。其中华盛顿邮报浮盈 214%、Kaiser Industries 浮盈 676%——都是 1973-1974 年熊市中买入的。 patience pays。

Berkshire Fine Spinning Associates and Hathaway Manufacturing were merged in 1955 to form Berkshire Hathaway Inc. In 1948, on a pro forma combined basis, they had earnings after tax of almost $18 million and employed 10,000 people at a dozen large mills throughout New England. In the business world of that period they were an economic powerhouse. For example, in that same year earnings of IBM were $28 million, Safeway Stores $10 million, Minnesota Mining $13 million, and Time, Inc. $9 million. But, in the decade following the 1955 merger aggregate sales of $595 million produced an aggregate loss for Berkshire Hathaway of $10 million.

1948 年 Berkshire 纺织赚 $1800 万——和 IBM（$2800 万）同一级别。10 年后合并亏损 $1000 万。这个对比令人震惊：一个和 IBM 同级别的企业，因为行业变迁而衰退到濒临破产。纺织业是"创造性破坏"的典型案例——不是管理不好，是行业被淘汰了。1948 年 IBM 还不是科技巨头，Safeway 还在早期——但它们在上升的行业里，Berkshire 在下降的行业里。

巴菲特讲这个故事不是自嘲——而是说明"不要被单年快照误导"。1948 年的 Berkshire 看起来很强（$1800 万利润），但已经在走下坡路。同理，1977 年的 Berkshire 看起来不大（$2190 万利润），但在走上坡路。关键不在当前规模，在趋势和方向。这也回应了前面的"顺风 vs 逆风"——选对行业比做大企业更重要。

Capital Cities Communications — "Better Management Through Non-Control"

Capital Cities possesses both extraordinary properties and extraordinary management. And these management skills extend equally to operations and employment of corporate capital. To purchase, directly, properties such as Capital Cities owns would cost in the area of twice our cost of purchase via the stock market, and direct ownership would offer no important advantages to us. While control would give us the opportunity - and the responsibility - to manage operations and corporate resources, we would not be able to provide management in either of those respects equal to that now in place. In effect, we can obtain a better management result through non-control than control. This is an unorthodox view, but one we believe to be sound.

"不控股比控股管理得更好。"这是巴菲特最"异端"的观点之一。Capital Cities 的管理层（Tom Murphy）比巴菲特自己更擅长运营传媒业务——控股反而会让他"有责任但无能力"管理。通过小比例持股，巴菲特享受了 Murphy 的管理能力，而不需要承担管理责任。

这个逻辑后来在 1985-1986 年被推向极致：Berkshire 以 $5.5 亿帮助 Capital Cities 收购 ABC（美国广播公司）——巴菲特提供资本，Murphy 提供管理。这是"提供弹药让别人开枪"的模式。Murphy 后来被巴菲特称为"有史以来最好的管理者之一"。Capital Cities/ABC 在 1995 年被迪士尼收购，Berkshire 的投资翻了数倍。"不控股的控股"——通过信任和长期关系实现比法律控制更深的绑定。

See's Candies — The Teacher

Since See's was purchased by Blue Chip Stamps at the beginning of 1972, pre-tax operating earnings have grown from $4.2 million to $12.6 million with little additional capital investment. See's achieved this record while operating in an industry experiencing practically no unit growth.

See's Candies：$420 万→$1260 万，5 年 3 倍，几乎不需要新资本。这是"轻资产+品牌定价权"的极致体现。糖果行业几乎没有销量增长——但 See's 通过提价实现了利润 3 倍增长。消费者对 See's 品牌的忠诚度让提价不会丢失客户——这就是"经济商誉"的力量。

See's 教会了巴菲特一个改变投资哲学的教训：一个有品牌护城河的企业，可以在不投入大量资本的情况下持续增长利润。这和纺织业形成了鲜明对比——纺织需要不断投入资本维持竞争力，但利润微薄；See's 几乎不需要资本投入，利润却持续增长。巴菲特后来在 1983 年信中系统阐述了"经济商誉 vs 会计商誉"——直接来源于 See's 的经验。可口可乐、苹果的投资决策都可以追溯到 See's 的启发。$2500 万买入的 See's，50 年累计贡献了超过 $20 亿税前利润——是投资史上回报最高的单一投资之一。

Banking — Gene Abegg Succession

Gene Abegg formed the bank in 1931 with $250,000. In its first full year of operation, earnings amounted to $8,782. Since that time, no new capital has been contributed to the bank; on the contrary, since our purchase in 1969, dividends of $20 million have been paid. Earnings in 1977 amounted to $3.6 million... Late last year Gene, now 80 and still running a banking operation without peer, asked that a successor be brought in.

Gene Abegg 80 岁退休——$25 万 → $3.6 亿资产，46 年没注入新资本。1931 年用 $25 万创立银行，1977 年利润 $360 万，1969-1977 年向 Berkshire 分红 $2000 万。Abegg 的银行是"资本效率"的极致——不注入新资本，全靠内生利润增长。80 岁请来接班人 Peter Jeffrey——这是 Berkshire 早期"创始人传承"的又一个案例。

Abegg 的银行后来在 1980 年因《银行控股公司法》被剥离出 Berkshire。但 Abegg 建立的"极高资本效率+极低风险"的银行文化，深深影响了巴菲特对金融企业的评估标准。后来收购 GEICO 保险时，巴菲特用的就是 Abegg 的标准：不靠新资本增长、保持高流动性、坏账率远低于平均。

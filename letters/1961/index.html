Buffett Partnership Letter — 1961

沃伦·巴菲特 · 31岁 · 合伙基金 $7.2M · 810 Kiewit Plaza · 1962年1月24日

时代背景 · 1961

宏观经济强劲复苏，GDP增速7.3%，通胀1.7%
市场道指从616涨至731（+18.7%），肯尼迪行情
政治肯尼迪就任，4月猪湾事件，柏林墙8月修建
Berkshire基金+45.9%，Dempster Mill控股完成，三类投资框架首次提出

Our Performance in 1961

During 1961, the general market as measured by the Dow-Jones Industrial Average showed an over-all gain of 22.2% including dividends received through ownership of the Dow. The gain for all partnerships operating throughout the entire year, after all expenses of operation, but before payments to limited partners or accrual to the general partner, averaged 45.9%.

合伙基金 +45.9%，道指 +22.2%——超额 23.7 个百分点。这是合伙基金五年来的最大超额。但巴菲特在第一段就重申："预期不变——牛市中我们会落后。"1961 年是意外之喜，主要来自低估型持仓在牛市中的爆发（他后文会解释）。五年累计：合伙基金 +251%，道指 +74.3%。按 LP 口径（扣费后）：+181.6% vs +74.3%。年化约 23% vs 11.7%。

关键数据：1962 年初基金净资产 $717 万，巴菲特夫妇自有资金 $102.5 万（14.3%），亲戚合计 $78 万。他把自己的钱全放进了基金——"vicuna coat"笑话的背景是他父亲做经纪业务，但巴菲特不通过父亲下单，避免利益冲突。

A Word About Par

The outstanding item of importance in my selection of partners, as well as in my subsequent relations with them, has been the determination that we use the same yardstick. If my performance is poor, I expect partners to withdraw, and indeed, I should look for a new source of investment for my own funds. If performance is good, I am assured of doing splendidly, a state of affairs to which I am sure I can adjust.

The rub, then, is in being sure that we all have the same ideas of what is good and what is poor. I believe in establishing yardsticks prior to the act; retrospectively, almost anything can be made to look good in relation to something or other.

"先定标准，再看成绩。"这是对 LP 最重要的治理承诺。如果事后选基准（cherry-picking），任何基金都能找到自己跑赢的指数。巴菲特坚持事前用道指做基准，而且说"三年是最短检验期，最好是起止点位接近的时段"——意思是如果起点低终点高（大牛市），即使跑赢也不算本事；如果起点高终点低（大熊市），少亏才是真本事。

他还做了一个详细对比：35 只大型共同基金中 32 只在 1946-1960 年跑输道指。这不是为了炫耀，而是说明"跑赢道指"其实是高标准——大部分专业机构做不到。这个结论到今天仍然成立：标普指数在过去 20 年跑赢了 90% 以上的主动管理基金。

Our Method of Operation

Our avenues of investment break down into three categories. These categories have different behavior characteristics, and the way our money is divided among them will have an important effect on our results, relative to the Dow in any given year.

The first section consists of generally undervalued securities (hereinafter called "generals") where we have nothing to say about corporate policies and no timetable as to when the undervaluation may correct itself. Over the years, this has been our largest category of investment, and more money has been made here than in either of the other categories. We usually have fairly large positions (5% to 10% of our total assets) in each of five or six generals, with smaller positions in another ten or fifteen.

三类投资框架正式确立——这是巴菲特投资体系的核心架构。
第一类"Generals"（低估型）：5-6 只重仓（各 5-10%）+ 10-15 只小仓。没有催化时间表，靠价值回归赚钱。这类持仓和道指同涨同跌，但长期跑赢。安全边际来自"以极低价格买入没有光环的股票"。巴菲特坦言"买入时机比卖出时机好得多"——他不在意卖在最高点，只要在买入价和公允价值之间退出就满意。

The generals tend to behave market-wise very much in sympathy with the Dow. Just because something is cheap does not mean it is not going to go down. During abrupt downward movements in the market, this segment may very well go down percentage-wise just as much as the Dow. Over a period of years, I believe the generals will outperform the Dow, and during sharply advancing years like 1961, this is the section of our portfolio that turns in the best results. It is, of course, also the most vulnerable in a declining market.

"便宜不意味着不跌。"这是对"价值陷阱"风险的清醒认识。低估股在暴跌中和大盘跌得一样多——因为恐慌中投资者不分好坏地抛售一切。但区别在于：低估股跌完后会回来，高估股可能永远回不来。巴菲特后来在 2008 年信中重复了这一点："价格下跌不代表价值下降。"关键是你是否知道你持有的是什么。

Our second category consists of "work-outs." These are securities whose financial results depend on corporate action rather than supply and demand factors created by buyers and sellers of securities... This category will produce reasonably stable earnings from year to year, to a large extent irrespective of the course of the Dow. I believe in using borrowed money to offset a portion of our work-out portfolio since there is a high degree of safety in this category... Results, excluding the benefits derived from the use of borrowed money, usually fall in the 10% to 20% range. My self-imposed limit regarding borrowing is 25% of partnership net worth.

第二类"Work-outs"（套利型）：10-20% 年化回报，与大盘无关。套利依赖并购、清算等公司事件，不受市场情绪影响。巴菲特在这里首次披露他使用杠杆——但仅限于套利组合，上限 25% 净资产。这是极其保守的杠杆：(1) 套利结果高度确定；(2) 套利持仓时间短（几个月到一年）；(3) 即使交易失败，损失也有限。用杠杆放大确定性高的收益，而不是放大方向性赌注——这是杠杆的正确用法。巴菲特后来在 1987 年股灾、2008 年危机中都不用杠杆买低估股，但套利杠杆始终保留。

The final category is "control" situations where we either control the company or take a very large position and attempt to influence policies of the company. Such operations should definitely be measured on the basis of several years. In a given year, they may produce nothing as it is usually to our advantage to have the stock be stagnant market-wise for a long period while we are acquiring it. These situations, too, have relatively little in common with the behavior of the Dow.

第三类"Control"（控股型）：以年为单位衡量，与大盘脱钩。控股型的核心逻辑是"主动创造价值"——通过更换管理层、拆分资产、推动并购等方式释放被锁住的价值。巴菲特说建仓时"希望股价不动"——因为还在买。这和大部分投资者完全相反：大部分人是买了就希望涨。控股型策略需要耐心（Dempster Mill 从买入到控股花了 5 年）、资金（没有一定规模做不了）和能力（能评估企业价值、能找到改善方案）。

Dempster Mill Manufacturing Company

We are presently involved in the control of Dempster Mill Manufacturing Company of Beatrice, Nebraska. Our first stock was purchased as a generally undervalued security five years ago. A block later became available, and I went on the Board about four years ago. In August 1961, we obtained majority control, which is indicative of the fact that many of our operations are not exactly of the "overnight" variety.

Dempster is a manufacturer of farm implements and water systems with sales in 1961 of about $9 million. Operations have produced only nominal profits in relation to invested capital during recent years. This reflected a poor management situation, along with a fairly tough industry situation. Presently, consolidated net worth (book value) is about $4.5 million, or $75 per share, consolidated working capital about $50 per share, and at yearend we valued our interest at $35 per share.

Dempster Mill——从低估型变成控股型的典型案例。内布拉斯加州的农机具制造商，年销售 $900 万，账面价值 $75/股，但巴菲特买入均价仅 $28/股（折价 63%）。他花了 5 年从"被动持有低估股"变成"控股 70%"。估值 $35/股——低于账面价值但高于买入价，体现了"控股折价"（流动性差、无法在公开市场卖出）。

这个案例展示了低估型如何自然演变为控股型：如果股价一直不涨，巴菲特就继续买入直到控股，然后主动推动价值释放。如果股价提前涨了，他就卖出完成一笔低估型交易。两条路都赚钱——这就是"进可攻退可守"的结构。1962 年巴菲特将请来 Harry Bottle 做CEO，大刀阔斧裁员减库存，一年内把 Dempster 的估值从 $35 提到 $51。

The Question of Conservatism

Many people some years back thought they were behaving in the most conservative manner by purchasing medium or long-term municipal or government bonds. This policy has produced substantial market depreciation in many cases, and most certainly has failed to maintain or increase real buying power.

Conscious, perhaps overly conscious, of inflation, many people now feel that they are behaving in a conservative manner by buying blue chip securities almost regardless of price-earnings ratios, dividend yields, etc. Without the benefit of hindsight as in the bond example, I feel this course of action is fraught with danger. There is nothing at all conservative, in my opinion, about speculating as to just how high a multiplier a greedy and capricious public will put on earnings.

"保守"的两种伪定义被逐一击破。第一种：买债券=保守。但通胀下债券实际购买力缩水——1960 年代通胀开始加速，长期债券持有人实际亏损。第二种：买蓝筹=保守。但"不管 PE 多高都买蓝筹"实际上是在赌"贪婪的大众会给盈利更高的乘数"——这是投机，不是保守。

巴菲特对保守的定义完全不同："你的假设对不对、事实对不对、推理对不对——这才是保守的基础。"不是买什么品种决定你保不保守，而是你的分析过程决定。一只冷门小盘股可能比一只热门蓝筹更"保守"——如果前者以 3 倍 PE 交易而后者以 50 倍。这个洞见到今天仍然是价值投资的核心争议点。

You will not be right simply because a large number of people momentarily agree with you. You will not be right simply because important people agree with you. In many quarters the simultaneous occurrence of the two above factors is enough to make a course of action meet the test of conservatism.

You will be right, over the course of many transactions, if your hypotheses are correct, your facts are correct, and your reasoning is correct. True conservatism is only possible through knowledge and reason.

整封信中最深刻的段落之一。"人多就是对"和"大人物就是对"——巴菲特在 31 岁就识别出了从众效应和权威偏误。这两句话是对 1958-1961 年市场狂热的直接回应：当时所有人都在买成长股，分析师们一致看多，结果很多"共识保守"的蓝筹在 1962 年崩盘中腰斩。

"真正的保守只能通过知识和理性实现"——这不是口号，是方法论。意味着你必须独立研究、独立估值、独立判断，而不是依赖他人的结论。这也是为什么巴菲特不参加行业会议、不看分析师报告——他要避免被"共识"污染。

The Question of Size

Larger funds tug in two directions. From the standpoint of "passive" investments, where we do not attempt by the size of our investment to influence corporate policies, larger sums hurt results... However, in the case of control situations increased funds are a definite advantage. A "Sanborn Map" cannot be accomplished without the wherewithal. My definite belief is that the opportunities increase in this field as the funds increase. This is due to the sharp fall-off in competition as the ante mounts plus the important positive correlation that exists between increased size of company and lack of concentrated ownership of that company's stock.

规模的双刃剑——31 岁已经在思考。规模增大对被动投资不利（买入 10,000 股小盘股比 100 股难得多），但对控股投资有利（大公司竞争对手少、股权分散）。这是巴菲特第一次系统讨论规模对业绩的影响。他的结论：目前两者抵消，但如果情况变化会告知 LP。

这个预警在 1969 年兑现——基金规模达到 $1 亿时，巴菲特认为规模已经严重损害被动投资收益，且控股机会不足以填补，于是选择解散基金。从 1961 年的预警到 1969 年的行动，中间隔了 8 年——巴菲特给了自己充分的时间来确认判断。这不是冲动决定，而是长期观察后的理性退出。

And a Prediction

I think you can be quite sure that over the next ten years there are going to be a few years when the general market is plus 20% or 25%, a few when it is minus on the same order, and a majority when it is in between. I haven't any notion as to the sequence in which these will occur, nor do I think it is of any great importance for the long-term investor.

Over any long period of years, I think it likely that the Dow will probably produce something like 5% to 7% per year compounded from a combination of dividends and market value gain. Despite the experience of recent years, anyone expecting substantially better than that from the general market probably faces disappointment.

道指长期年化 5-7%——这个预测精准得惊人。1961-1971 年道指年化约 5.5%（含分红）。巴菲特不是说未来一年会怎样（他反复说不知道），而是说十年的框架。他不预测短期，但基于估值水平判断长期回报区间——这是格雷厄姆教给他的方法：市场估值高于历史均值时，未来回报大概率低于均值。

他给自己设定的目标：年化跑赢道指 10 个百分点，即 15-17%。实际 1957-1969 年合伙基金年化约 29.5%，超额约 22%——远超目标。但巴菲特在 1961 年说"这些预期可能听起来有些鲁莽"——他不给自己的目标打保票，只是让 LP 知道他的思维方式。

Specifically, if the market should be down 35% or 40% in a year (and I feel this has a high probability of occurring one year in the next ten--no one knows which one), we should be down only 15% or 20%. If it is more or less unchanged during the year, we would hope to be up about ten percentage points. If it is up 20% or more, we would struggle to be up as much.

三种情景的预测全部应验。巴菲特给出未来十年的操作蓝图：大熊市（道指 -35~40%）时基金 -15~20%；平盘时基金 +10%；大牛市（道指 +20%+）时基金勉强跟上。实际验证：
1962 年道指 -10%，基金 +13.9%（平盘偏熊，超额 24%）
1966 年道指 -15%，基金 +20.7%（熊市大幅跑赢）
1968 年道指 +7.7%，基金 +58.8%（牛市意外大超）

"未来十年有一年会跌 35-40%"——这个预测在 1973-1974 年应验（道指两年累计跌约 45%），但那时巴菲特已解散合伙基金。如果他还在管基金，那两年大概率就是他说的"基金只跌 15-20%"——然后是 1975 年的报复性反弹。这种"先说后做"的透明度，是巴菲特能保持 LP 信任 13 年的根本原因。

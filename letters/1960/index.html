Buffett Partnership Letter — 1960

沃伦·巴菲特 · 30岁 · 七个合伙基金 · 奥马哈 · 1961年1月30日 + 7月22日半年报

时代背景 · 1960

宏观经济温和衰退（1960.4-1961.2），GDP增速2.6%，失业率5.5%
市场道指从679跌至616（-9.3%），选举年不确定
政治尼克松vs肯尼迪总统竞选，U-2侦察机事件，巴黎和谈破裂
Berkshire基金+25.9%，Sanborn Map完整案例（控股后释放价值）

The General Stock Market in 1960

In 1960, the picture was reversed. The Industrial Average declined from 679 to 616, or 9.3%. Adding back the dividends which would have been received through ownership of the Average still left it with an overall loss of 6.3%. On the other hand, the Utility Average showed a good gain and, while all the results are not now available, my guess is that about 90% of all investment companies outperformed the Industrial Average. The majority of investment companies appear to have ended the year with overall results in the range of plus or minus 5%. On the New York Stock Exchange, 653 common stocks registered losses for the year while 404 showed gains.

1960 年道指 -6.3%，但 90% 的投资公司跑赢了道指。这和 1959 年正好相反——1959 年道指涨 20% 但大多数基金跑不赢。原因：1960 年大盘股（道指成分股）拖累，而中小盘和公用事业股表现不错。这说明道指作为基准的缺陷：它只反映 30 只大盘股，不代表市场全貌。巴菲特连续两年指出这个"失真"，为的是让 LP 理解：跑赢道指不难（基金经常做到），但持续大幅跑赢才难。1960 年 NYSE 653 只下跌 vs 404 只上涨——这其实是结构性熊市，不是温和调整。

Results in 1960

My continual objective in managing partnership funds is to achieve a long-term performance record superior to that of the Industrial Average. I believe this Average, over a period of years, will more or less parallel the results of leading investment companies. Unless we do achieve this superior performance there is no reason for existence of the partnerships.

However, I have pointed out that any superior record which we might accomplish should not be expected to be evidenced by a relatively constant advantage in performance compared to the Average. Rather it is likely that if such an advantage is achieved, it will be through better-than-average performance in stable or declining markets and average, or perhaps even poorer-than-average performance in rising markets.

"如果跑不赢道指，合伙基金就没有存在理由。"这是巴菲特对自己最严苛的承诺。注意他进一步细化预期：超额收益不是均匀分布的，而是在不同市场环境中不对称地体现。这就像一个棒球选手——整体打击率高于平均，但对左投手特别好、对右投手可能略低于平均。巴菲特说"不要期待每年稳定跑赢 5%"，而是"熊市大幅跑赢、牛市可能跑输"。

四年累计数据已经很说明问题：合伙基金累计 +140.6%，道指 +42.6%。年化超额约 20%。但巴菲特坚持说"四年太短"。他后来在 1964 年信中给出了完整的五年/十年检验框架。

I would consider a year in which we declined 15% and the Average 30% to be much superior to a year when both we and the Average advanced 20%. Over a period of time there are going to be good and bad years; there is nothing to be gained by getting enthused or depressed about the sequence in which they occur. The important thing is to be beating par; a four on a par three hole is not as good as a five on a par five hole and it is unrealistic to assume we are not going to have our share of both par three's and par five's.

高尔夫比喻精准传达相对收益思维。"par three"=容易的年份（牛市），"par five"=困难的年份（熊市）。巴菲特说：在难年份少亏比在易年份多赚更重要。这是因为复利的不对称性——亏损 50% 需要盈利 100% 才能回本。所以"跌 15% 而道指跌 30%"虽然绝对亏损，但相对跑赢 15 个百分点，长期复利效果远优于"都涨 20%"。

"不要因年份的排列顺序而兴奋或沮丧"——这是对抗行为偏差的建议。如果先牛市再熊市，LP 可能在牛市觉得巴菲特不行（跑不赢），熊市才恍然大悟。反之亦然。关键是长期看总成绩，而不是单年排名。

Sanborn Map

Last year mention was made of an investment which accounted for a very high and unusual proportion (35%) of our net assets along with the comment that I had some hope this investment would be concluded in 1960. This hope materialized. The history of an investment of this magnitude may be of interest to you.

Sanborn Map Co. is engaged in the publication and continuous revision of extremely detailed maps of all cities of the United States. For example, the volumes mapping Omaha would weigh perhaps fifty pounds and provide minute details on each structure. The map would be revised by the paste-over method showing new construction, changed occupancy, new fire protection facilities, changed structural materials, etc.

Sanborn Map——巴菲特合伙基金时期最重要的案例。这家公司为火灾保险公司制作超详细的城市地图（奥马哈一本地图重 50 磅），标注水管直径、消防栓位置、屋顶材质等。75 年近乎垄断经营，不需要销售，不受衰退影响——完美的"护城河"业务。但 1950 年代出现了"carding"新核保技术，地图业务利润从年 $50 万跌到不足 $10 万。

巴菲特看到的是：地图业务虽然衰退，但底层资产（地图数据本身）仍然有价值——重新积累这些数据要花几千万美元。这和后来收购《华盛顿邮报》、《布法罗新闻报》的逻辑一致：传统媒介看似过时，但信息本身有持久价值，关键是找到新的变现方式。

However, during the early 1930's Sanborn had began to accumulate an investment portfolio. There were no capital requirements to the business so that any retained earnings could be devoted to this project. Over a period of time, about $2.5 million was invested, roughly half in bonds and half in stocks. Thus, in the last decade particularly, the investment portfolio blossomed while the operating map business wilted.

Let me give you some idea of the extreme divergence of these two factors. In 1938 when the Dow-Jones Industrial Average was in the 100-120 range, Sanborn sold at $110 per share. In 1958 with the Average in the 550 area, Sanborn sold at $45 per share. Yet during that same period the value of the Sanborn investment portfolio increased from about $20 per share to $65 per share. This means, in effect, that the buyer of Sanborn stock in 1938 was placing a positive valuation of $90 per share on the map business ($110 less the $20 value of the investments unrelated to the map business) in a year of depressed business and stock market conditions. In the tremendously more vigorous climate of 1958 the same map business was evaluated at a minus $20 with the buyer of the stock unwilling to pay more than 70 cents on the dollar for the investment portfolio with the map business thrown in for nothing.

教科书级的价值投资案例。1938 年：股价 $110 = $20 投资 + $90 地图业务。1958 年：股价 $45 = $65 投资 - $20 地图业务。市场给地图业务的估值从 +$90 变成 -$20，同时投资组合从 $20 涨到 $65——意味着你花 $45 买到了价值 $65 的证券组合，地图业务白送还倒贴 $20。

为什么会出现这种荒谬的定价？因为 Sanborn 太小、太无聊，没有分析师覆盖，没有机构关注。300 个股东，每月两笔交易——市场定价机制基本失效。巴菲特的优势不是信息优势（财报公开），而是"愿意去看、愿意去算"。在 1958-1959 年，他用约 $51/股的均价买入了 24,000 股（约 23% 的公司），然后联合其他大股东推动改革。

There was considerable opposition on the Board to change of any type, particularly when initiated by an outsider, although management was in complete accord with our plan and a similar plan had been recommended by Booz, Allen & Hamilton (Management Experts). To avoid a proxy fight (which very probably would not have been forthcoming and which we would have been certain of winning) and to avoid time delay with a large portion of Sanborn's money tied up in blue-chip stocks which I didn't care for at current prices, a plan was evolved taking out all stockholders at fair value who wanted out. The SEC ruled favorably on the fairness of the plan. About 72% of the Sanborn stock, involving 50% of the 1,600 stockholders, was exchanged for portfolio securities at fair value.

解决方案的精妙之处。巴菲特没有发动代理权争夺（虽然他能赢），而是设计了一个"自愿退出"方案：想退出的股东可以按公允价值换取 Sanborn 投资组合中的蓝筹股。72% 的股票退出，50% 的股东拿走证券离场。结果：(1) 想走的人走了，拿到了公允价值；(2) 留下的人每股盈利和分红大增；(3) 地图业务保留 $125 万债券作为储备；(4) 消除了 $100 万潜在资本利得税。多赢。

巴菲特后来很少用这种"拆分投资组合"的方式——因为随着基金规模增大，这类小公司已不值得操作。但 Sanborn 案例确立了一个原则：当市场不给公允估值时，大股东可以通过公司行动创造价值。这个思路贯穿了 Dempster Mill（1962）、Berkshire（1965）和后来的所有控股操作。

Necessarily, the above little melodrama is a very abbreviated description of this investment operation. However, it does point up the necessity for secrecy regarding our portfolio operations as well as the futility of measuring our results over a short span of time such as a year. Such control situations may occur very infrequently. Our bread-and-butter business is buying undervalued securities and selling when the undervaluation is corrected along with investment in special situations where the profit is dependent on corporate rather than market action. To the extent that partnership funds continue to grow, it is possible that more opportunities will be available in "control situations."

"control situations"（控股型机会）正式成为第三类策略。至此巴菲特的投资框架完整了：(1) 低估型（generals）——赚价值回归的钱；(2) 套利型（work-outs）——赚公司事件的钱；(3) 控股型（control）——赚主动创造价值的钱。三类策略资金分配随市场环境动态调整：熊市多配低估，牛市多配套利，机会出现时重仓控股。

"资金继续增长，控股机会可能更多"——这句话预示了 1960 年代巴菲特的投资重心从"被动等待价值回归"转向"主动推动价值释放"。Dempster Mill（1962）、Berkshire（1965）、Hochschild-Kohn（1966）都是这一思路的延续。但巴菲特也坦承这类机会"非常罕见"——他不会因为想控股而降低标准。

Semi-Annual Letter — July 1961

During the first half of 1961, the overall gain of the Dow-Jones Industrial Average was about 13%, including dividends. Although this is the type of period when we should have the most difficulty in exceeding this standard, all partnerships that operated throughout the six months did moderately better then the Average.

Let me, however, emphasize two points. First, one year is far too short a period to form any kind of an opinion as to investment performance, and measurements based upon six months become even more unreliable. My own thinking is much more geared to five year performance, preferably with tests of relative results in both strong and weak markets.

"五年是最短的评估周期。"这是巴菲特最重要的治理原则之一。1961 年上半年牛市，巴菲特跑赢了一点——他立刻警告不要因此乐观。半年报的存在本身就让他不安："担心 LP 开始用短期业绩思考。"他明确说自己的思维框架是五年起步，最好是经历牛市和熊市两个完整周期。

这个"五年法则"贯穿了巴菲特的整个职业生涯。1988 年他说"如果要评估我的表现，至少看五年"；2014 年 50 周年信中仍说"不要用一年衡量我们"。对短期业绩的漠视，正是长期超额收益的前提条件——因为追求短期排名必然导致追涨杀跌。

The second point I want everyone to understand is that if we continue in a market which advances at the pace of the first half of 1961, not only do I doubt that we will continue to exceed the results of the DJIA, but it is very likely that our performance will fall behind the Average. Our holdings, which I always believe to be on the conservative side compared to general portfolios, tend to grow more conservative as the general market level rises. At all times, I attempt to have a portion of our portfolio in securities as least partially insulated from the behavior of the market, and this portion should increase as the market rises. However appetizing results for even the amateur cook (and perhaps particularly the amateur), we find that more of our portfolio is not on the stove.

越涨越保守。牛市中巴菲特不是追涨，而是反向加仓套利和低估防御型标的。比喻精妙："对业余厨师来说最香的菜，对我们来说越来越多不在炉子上"——意思是市场涨得越猛，他的组合越"不在市场中"。这不是踏空，而是纪律：涨多了风险就大了，该减仓就减仓。1961 年下半年市场确实回调，巴菲特的保守再次被证明是对的。

这种"越涨越退"的策略需要极强的心理素质——因为牛市中你的排名必然下降，LP 会质疑你"为什么不多买"。巴菲特用半年报提前管理预期，把"跑不赢"说成策略特征而非缺陷。

We have also begun open market acquisition of a potentially major commitment which I, of course, hope does nothing marketwise for at least a year. Such a commitment may be a deterrent to short range performance, but it gives strong promise of superior results over a several year period combined with substantial defensive characteristics.

Progress has been made toward combining all partners at yearend... Estimated total assets of the partnership will be in the neighborhood of $4 million, which enables us to consider investments such as the one mentioned earlier in this letter, which we would have had to pass several years ago.

两个重要信号。第一，新的"大型持仓"在建仓中——这很可能是 Dempster Mill（农机具制造商），1961 年底成为合伙基金第二大持仓。巴菲特说"希望股价至少一年不动"——典型的建仓期心态，和 1957 年说的"sterile stage"一脉相承。第二，基金规模增长到约 $400 万，使得他可以操作更大标的。规模是一把双刃剑：更多机会，但也更难跑赢——这个矛盾在 1969 年达到顶点，巴菲特因"规模太大找不到便宜货"而解散基金。

同时，巴菲特宣布合并所有合伙基金为单一实体，统一费率为"6% hurdle + 25% carry"——这就是后来成为对冲基金行业标准的"2-and-20"的原型（只不过巴菲特没有管理费，只有业绩提成）。他还承诺自己全家所有可投资资产都在基金里，不另设账户——利益完全绑定。

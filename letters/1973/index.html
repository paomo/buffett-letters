1973 Letter

To the Stockholders of Berkshire Hathaway Inc.:

Our financial results for 1973 were satisfactory, with operating earnings of $11,930,592, producing a return of 17.4% on beginning stockholders’ equity. Although operating earnings improved from $11.43 to $12.18 per share, earnings on equity decreased from the 19.8% of 1972. This decline occurred because the gain in earnings was not commensurate with the increase in shareholders’ investment. We had forecast in last year’s report that such a decline was likely. Unfortunately, our forecast proved to be correct.

ROE 从 19.8% 降到 17.4%——"不幸地，我们的预测是对的"。1973 年是熊市开端：道指从 1051 跌到 851（-19%），石油危机爆发，通胀加速。Berkshire 利润仍在增长（$11.43→$12.18/股），但增速低于资本增速——因为 1972 年借了 $2000 万注入保险子公司，资本基数扩大了。

1973 年的背景极其重要：这是 1973-1974 年大熊市的起点。巴菲特在这年开始大量买入股票——最著名的是华盛顿邮报。他年初开始买入 WPO，到年底已持有约 10% 股份。但 1973 年底组合浮亏超过 $1200 万——"低买还在更低"。巴菲特不为所动，1974 年继续加仓。这段经历后来被他在多次信中引用：即使你看对了企业价值，市场可能持续低估数年。耐心是价值投资者唯一的武器。

Our textile, banking, and most insurance operations had good years, but certain segments of the insurance business turned in poor results. Overall, our insurance business continues to be a most attractive area in which to employ capital.

业务板块冷热不均：纺织、银行和多数保险业务不错，但保险的某些细分拖了后腿。注意巴菲特的定调——保险整体仍是"最吸引人的资本运用领域"。此后几十年，保险浮存金（float）一直是伯克希尔的发动机之一；1973 年信里这句话是这个判断最早的白纸黑字。

Management’s objective is to achieve a return on capital over the long term which averages somewhat higher than that of American industry generally—while utilizing sound accounting and debt policies. We have achieved this goal in the last few years, and are trying to take those steps which will enable us to maintain this performance in the future. Prospects for 1974 indicate some further decline in rate of return on our enlarged capital base.

长期目标正式化：ROE 高于美国工业平均水平。"同时使用稳健的会计和债务政策。"——巴菲特的目标不是最大化短期回报，而是可持续的长期超额回报。1974 年他预期回报率继续下降。

Textile Operations

Textile demand remained unusually strong throughout 1973. Our main problems revolved around shortages of fiber, which complicated operations and resulted in something less than full utilization of loom capacity. Prices of some fibers skyrocketed during the year.

纺织业 1973 年需求强但纤维短缺，价格管控限制了定价。巴菲特改用 LIFO（后进先出）存货计价——"更接近当前成本对当前收入，最小化存货利润"。这是会计保守主义的体现：不把通胀带来的存货涨价算成利润。

Cost of Living Council regulations prevented the pricing of many finished products at levels of some of our competitors. However, profits were reasonably commensurate with our capital investment, although below those that apparently might have been achieved had we been able to price at market levels. The textile business has been highly cyclical and price controls may have served to cut down some of the hills while still leaving us with the inevitable valleys.

"价格管控削掉了一些山丘，却给我们留下了不可避免的山谷。"1971-1974 年尼克松政府的工资-价格管控（Cost of Living Council）下，纺织成品无法随行就市定价，利润低于本可达到的水平。巴菲特点出周期股的宿命：管制只能削峰、不能填谷。他刻意用"与资本投入相称"（reasonably commensurate）作标尺——这门生意长期跑不赢资本成本，为 1985 年最终关闭纺织厂埋下伏笔。

Because of the extraordinary price rises in raw materials during 1973, which show signs of continuing in 1974, we have elected to adopt the “lifo” method of inventory pricing. This method more nearly matches current costs against current revenues, and minimizes inventory

“profits” included in reported earnings. Further information on this change is included in the footnotes to our financial statements.

During 1973, Jack Ringwalt retired as President of National Indemnity Company after an absolutely brilliant record since founding the business in 1940. He was succeeded by Phil

Jack Ringwalt 退休——1940 年创办 National Indemnity 的传奇。Phil Liesche 接任，拥有相同的承保和管理哲学。巴菲特最看重的是"哲学传承"——保险业的成败在承保纪律，不在规模。

Liesche who, fortunately for us, possesses the same underwriting and managerial philosophy that worked so well for Jack.

Our traditional business, specialized auto and general liability lines conducted through National

传统业务 1973 年承保优秀但量继续降——竞争激烈，巴菲特拒绝跟风砍价。"有一些竞争者开始意识到费率和损失准备金的不足。"——反周期等待的耐心开始得到回报的迹象。

Indemnity Company and National Fire and Marine Insurance Company, had an exceptionally fine underwriting year during 1973. We again experienced a decline in volume. Competition was intense, and we passed up the chance to match rate‐cutting by more optimistic underwriters. There currently are faint indications that some of these competitors are learning of the inadequacy of their rates (and also of their loss reserves) which may result in easing of market pressures as the year develops. If so, we may again experience volume increases.

Our reinsurance operation had a somewhat similar year—good underwriting experience, but difficulty in maintaining previous volume levels. This operation, guided by the tireless and well‐ directed efforts of George Young, has been a major profit producer since its inception in 1969.

再保险业务自 1969 年创立就是重要利润来源，George Young"不知疲倦、方向正确"的努力功不可没。1973 年承保良好但量难维持——和传统业务一样，宁可不做也不降价抢单。巴菲特对人的判断极简：把对的生意交给对的人，然后放手让他干。

Our “home state” insurance companies made excellent progress in Nebraska and Minnesota, with both good growth in volume and acceptable loss ratios. We began operations late in the year in Iowa. To date, our big problem has been Texas. In that state we virtually had to start over during 1973 as the initial management we selected proved incapable of underwriting successfully. The Texas experience has been expensive, and we still have our work cut out for us. Overall, however, the home state operation appears to have a promising potential.

本州模式：内布拉斯加和明尼苏达进步良好（量增+损失率可接受），年底进入爱荷华。但德州是灾难——初始管理层"承保无能"，几乎推倒重来。"德州经验代价高昂，我们还有工作要做。"

Our specialized urban auto operation, Home and Automobile Insurance Company, experienced very poor underwriting in Chicago during 1973. It would appear that rates are inadequate in our primary Cook County marketing area, although the current energy situation confuses the picture. The question is whether possible lowered accident frequency because of reduced driving will more than offset continuing inflation in medical and repair costs, as well as jury awards. We believe that inflation will hurt us more than reduced driving will help us, but some of our competitors appear to believe otherwise.

Home & Auto 1973 年芝加哥承保很差。核心问题：费率不足 + 通胀（医疗和维修成本、陪审团判决上涨）vs 能源危机减少驾驶。巴菲特判断"通胀的伤害大于驾驶减少的帮助"——但竞争者不这么认为。另外暴露管理缺陷：会计系统信息不及时。

Home and Auto expanded into Florida and California during the year, but it is too early to know how these moves will prove out financially.

管理会计缺陷被点名：信息不及时。Home & Auto 1973 年业绩差，巴菲特把一部分责任归给会计系统——管理层拿不到及时数据。注意他用的词是"contributing factor"（因素之一）而非全部原因，归因审慎。差的报表系统本身就是经营风险，这也是他后来年报里反复强调管理质量的先声。

1973 年大量加仓股票，年末浮亏超 $1200 万。但巴菲特说："按成本计的股票组合，就内在商业价值而言代表好价值。"——这是 1973-74 熊市中的逆势加仓（华盛顿邮报、See's 母公司 Blue Chip 等）。"尽管年末浮亏巨大，长期预期满意。"

Banking Operations

The Illinois National Bank & Trust Co. of Rockford again had a record year in 1973. Average deposits were approximately $130 million, of which approximately 60% were time deposits.

伊利诺伊银行 1973 年再创纪录：存款约 $1.3 亿（60% 定期），税后回报率仍超 2.1%（含新增伊利诺伊州税）。Gene Abegg 1931 年创办以来一直经营这家银行——巴菲特钦佩的"一生经营一家企业"。

Interest rates were increased substantially in the important consumer savings area when regulatory maximums were raised at mid‐year.

Despite this mix heavily weighted toward interest bearing deposits, our operating earnings after taxes (including a new Illinois state income tax) were again over 2.1% of average deposits.

We continue to be the largest bank in Rockford. We continue to maintain unusual liquidity. We continue to meet the increasing loan demands of our customers. And we continue to maintain our unusual profitability. This is a direct tribute to the abilities of Gene Abegg, Chairman, who has been running the Bank since it opened its doors in 1931, and Bob Kline, our President.

Your Directors have approved the merger of Diversified Retailing Company, Inc. into Berkshire

DRC 并入 Berkshire——结构简化。195,000 股 Berkshire 换 100 万股 DRC，净增仅 85,449 股。DRC 最重要的资产：Blue Chip Stamps 的 16% 股权（通过 See's Candies 和 Wesco 拥有盈利来源）。

Hathaway Inc. on terms involving issuance of 195,000 shares of Berkshire stock for the 1,000,000 shares of Diversified stock outstanding. Because Diversified and its subsidiaries own 109,551 shares of Berkshire, the net increase in the number of shares of Berkshire outstanding after giving effect to this transaction will not exceed 85,449. Various regulatory approvals must be obtained before this merger can be completed, and proxy material will be submitted to you later this year so that you may vote upon it. Diversified Retailing Company, Inc., though subsidiaries, operates a chain of popular‐priced women’s apparel stores and also conducts a reinsurance business. In the opinion of management, its most important asset is 16% of the stock of Blue Chip Stamps.

Blue Chip Stamps

Our holdings of stock in Blue Chip Stamps at year‐end amounted to approximately 19% of that company’s outstanding shares. Since year‐end, we have increased our holdings so that they now represent approximately 22.5%: implementation of the proposed merger with Diversified

Blue Chip Stamps 成为关键棋子。从 19% 增持到 22.5%，DRC 合并后约 38.5%。交易印花业务暴跌，但 See's Candies 和 Wesco（54% 储蓄贷款）提供盈利来源。这是巴菲特搭建伯克希尔帝国拼图的关键一步。

Retailing Company, Inc. would increase this figure to about 38.5%.

Our equity in earnings of Blue Chip Stamps became significant for the first time in 1973, and posed an accounting question as to just what period’s earnings should be recognized by Berkshire Hathaway Inc. as applicable to the financial statements covered by this annual report.

Blue Chip’s fiscal year ends on the Saturday closest to February 28, or two months after the fiscal year‐end of Berkshire Hathaway Inc. Or, viewed alternatively, their year ends ten months prior to Berkshire Hathaway’s. An acceptable accounting choice for us, and one which, if made, would not have required an auditor’s disclaimer as to scope, was to recognize in our 1973 income an equity of $632,000 in Blue Chip’s earnings for their year ended March 3, 1973 with regard to the fewer shares of Blue Chip we owned during this earlier period. But such an approach seemed at odds with reality, and would have meant a ten month lag each year in the future. Therefore, we chose to reflect as 1973 income our equity of $1,008,000 in Blue Chip’s earnings based upon unaudited interim earnings through November as publicly reported by Blue Chip Stamps and with regard to our shareholdings during 1973. Because we made this choice of unaudited but current figures, as opposed to the alternative of audited but far from current figures, Peat, Marwick, Mitchell & Co. were unable to express an opinion on our 1973 earnings attributable to Blue Chip Stamps.

为"实质重于形式"，宁要未经审计的当期数字，不要已审计的滞后数字。Blue Chip 财年比 Berkshire 早结束两个月（或说滞后十个月）。选项 A：确认已审计的 $632,000，但从此每年都要背十个月的滞后；选项 B：确认未经审计的当期 $1,008,000。巴菲特选了 B，代价是审计师 Peat, Marwick, Mitchell 对这部分收益无法出具意见——这是"穿透收益"（look-through earnings）思想的雏形：会计期间应反映真实的经济归属，而非机械地迁就审计日历。风险面：无保留意见的缺失会吓跑保守的股东与债权人，这是他为信息及时性付出的明确代价。

Blue Chip’s trading stamp business has declined drastically over the past year or so, but it has important sources of earning power in its See’s Candy Shops subsidiary as well as Wesco

Financial Corporation, a 54% owned subsidiary engaged in the savings and loan business. We expect Blue Chip Stamps to achieve satisfactory earnings in future years related to capital employed, although certainly at a much lower level than would have been achieved if the trading stamp business had been maintained at anything close to former levels.

印花业务断崖，See's 和 Wesco 顶上。交易印花（trading stamps）生意过去一年多急剧萎缩，Blue Chip 未来的盈利靠 See's 糖果和 Wesco（54% 持股的储贷公司）。巴菲特已经看清：真正值钱的是 See's 这种有定价权的消费品牌，而非靠折扣兑换存活的印花票。1972 年买入 See's 时它已是 Blue Chip 子公司——这笔"买贵了的优秀生意"日后成为巴菲特复利叙事的核心案例。

Your Chairman is on the Board of Directors of Blue Chip Stamps, as well as Wesco Financial

Corporation, and is Chairman of the Board of See’s Candy Shops Incorporated. Operating management of all three entities is in the hands of first‐class, able, experienced executives.

Sun Newspapers, Inc.

In the 1969 annual report we commented on the purchase of Sun Newspapers Inc., a group of weekly papers published in the metropolitan Omaha area. Since that time we have not commented on their operations in the text of our annual reports, nor have we consolidated their financial results since the operation, because of the small investment involved, has been

“financially insignificant.”

会计上的"财务重要性"（materiality）与质量上的重要性是两回事：1969 年买的奥马哈周报群投资额太小，连合并报表都免了；但 1973 年它拿下普利策奖。"规模不等于意义"——巴菲特用这个小例子提醒股东：账面数字衡量不了所有价值。

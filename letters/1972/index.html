1972 Letter

To the Stockholders of Berkshire Hathaway Inc.:

Operating earnings of Berkshire Hathaway during 1972 amounted to a highly satisfactory 19.8% of beginning shareholders’ equity. Significant improvement was recorded in all of our major lines of business, but the most dramatic gains were in insurance underwriting profit. Due to an unusual convergence of favorable factors—diminishing auto accident frequency, moderating accident severity, and an absence of major catastrophes—underwriting profit margins achieved a level far above averages of the past or expectations of the future.

ROE 19.8%，每股账面价值 8 年年化 16.5%——没有注入一分钱新资本。这是 Berkshire 模式的第一次完整验证：1965 年接手时账面价值 $2213 万，1972 年 $1111 万利润——如果全留在纺织，利润可能只有几百万。通过把纺织现金流转入保险和银行，Berkshire 的盈利能力提升了数倍。

关键数据：1964 年每股账面 $19.46 → 1972 年 $69.72，年化 16.5%。而且"没有注入新资本，反而回购了 14% 股本"——这意味着所有增长都来自内生利润再投资。巴菲特后来在 1983 年引入"每股内在价值"概念，认为它比账面价值更好地反映 Berkshire 的真实价值。但在 1972 年，账面价值已经是衡量"资本重新配置"效果的好指标。

While we anticipate a modest decrease in operating earnings during 1973, it seems clear that our diversification moves of recent years have established a significantly higher base of normal earning power. Your present management assumed policy control of the company in May, 1965. Eight years later, our 1972 operating earnings of $11,116,256 represent a return many‐ fold higher than would have been produced had we continued to devote our resources entirely to the textile business. At the end of the 1964 fiscal year, shareholders’ equity totaled

多元化成果量化：8 年前后对比。1964 年股东权益 $2214 万，到 1972 年 $6972 万/股（年化 16.5% 增长）——期间没有新增一分钱股本，还回购了 14% 的股票。如果继续死守纺织业，"回报会低很多倍"。这就是资本再部署的力量。

$22,138,753. Since that time, no additional equity capital has been introduced into the business, either through cash sale or through merger. On the contrary, some stock has been reacquired, reducing outstanding shares by 14%. The increase in book value per share from $19.46 at fiscal year‐end 1964 to $69.72 at 1972 year‐end amounts to about 16.5% compounded annually.

Our three major acquisitions of recent years have all worked out exceptionally well—from both the financial and human standpoints. In all three cases, the founders were major sellers and received significant proceeds in cash—and, in all three cases, the same individuals, Jack

三个收购的共同点：创始人卖身不卖心。Jack Ringwalt（National Indemnity）、Gene Abegg（伊利诺伊银行）、Vic Raab（Home & Auto）——都是拿了现金但继续用不减的精力经营。巴菲特后来反复强调：买公司就是买人。

Ringwalt, Gene Abegg and Vic Raab, have continued to run the businesses with undiminished energy and imagination which have resulted in further improvement of the fine records previously established.

We will continue to search for logical extensions of our present operations, and also for new operations which will allow us to continue to employ our capital effectively.

资本再配置的宣言：“继续寻找现有业务的合理延伸和新业务”。1972 年 1 月 Berkshire 刚通过 Blue Chip 以 $2500 万收购 See's Candies——但这封信只字未提，因为当时 Berkshire 仅持股 Blue Chip 36%，尚未并表。这正说明巴菲特眼中“资本雇佣”永远是动态的：纺织赚的现金流向保险和糖果，哪里有高回报就去哪里。后来 1980 年代他反复强调“资本配置是管理层最重要的工作”，此处已见雏形。

Textile Operations

As predicted in last year’s annual report, the textile industry experienced a pickup in 1972. In recent years, Ken Chace and Ralph Rigby have developed an outstanding sales organization enjoying a growing reputation for service and reliability. Manufacturing capabilities have been restructured to complement our sales strengths.

纺织业 1972 年复苏，Ken Chace 和 Ralph Rigby 的销售组织是功臣。但巴菲特始终清醒："行业整体盈利水平永远是决定我们纺织利润的首要因素。"——个体的努力只能改善相对位置，改变不了行业命运。

Helped by the industry recovery, we experienced some payoff from these efforts in 1972.

Inventories were controlled, minimizing close‐out losses in addition to minimizing capital requirements; product mix was greatly improved. While the general level of profitability of the industry will always be the primary factor in determining the level of our textile earnings, we believe that our relative position within the industry has noticeably improved. The outlook for 1973 is good.

纺织的“相对改善”：库存受控、产品结构优化，但行业盈利水平仍是决定因素。巴菲特清醒地把“行业 beta”和“公司 alpha”分开：行业复苏是主因，管理改善只是让 Berkshire 在同业中的相对位次提升。1972 年他仍不愿承认纺织是资本陷阱，直到 1985 年才正式关闭纺织业务——这段反复投入又反复失望的历史，正是后来“好行业比好管理更重要”（1989 年致股东信）论点的反面教材。

Insurance Underwriting

Our exceptional underwriting profits during 1972 in the large traditional area of our insurance business at National Indemnity present a paradox. They served to swell substantially total corporate profits for 1972, but the factors which produced such profits induced exceptional amounts of new competition at what we believe to be a non‐compensatory level of rates. Over‐ all, we probably would have retained better prospects for the next five years if profits had not risen so dramatically this year.

保险业的悖论：大赚反而损害未来。1972 年承保暴利引来大量竞争者以不合理的费率抢市场。"总体上，如果今年利润没有涨这么猛，我们未来五年的前景反而更好。"——巴菲特宁愿少赚也要保住长期的费率纪律。这就是反周期思维。

Substantial new competition was forecast in our annual report for last year and we experienced in 1972 the decline in premium volume that we stated such competition implied. Our belief is that industry underwriting profit margins will narrow substantially in 1973 or 1974 and, in time, this may produce an environment in which our historical growth can be resumed.

预言的兑现：竞争导致保费量下降。巴菲特预期行业承保利润率 1973-74 年大幅收窄，届时"我们的历史增长可以恢复"。但衰退和竞争退出之间存在时滞——这段期间传统业务会有负增长。

Unfortunately, there is a lag between deterioration of underwriting results and tempering of competition. During this period we expect to continue to have negative volume comparisons in our traditional operation. Our seasoned management, headed by Jack Ringwalt and Phil

Liesche, will continue to underwrite to produce a profit, although not at the level of 1972, and base our rates on long‐term expectations rather than short‐term hopes. Although this approach has meant dips in volume from time to time in the past, it has produced excellent long‐term results.

费率纪律：“基于长期预期定价，而不是短期希望”。在承保结果恶化与竞争退潮之间存在时滞，这段时间宁可忍受保费负增长，也不降价抢业务。这是巴菲特保险哲学的基石——后来在 1980、1992 等年份反复出现：“在别人贪婪时恐惧”在承保端的体现，就是拒绝非补偿性费率。短期丢规模，长期赢利润。

Also as predicted in last year’s report, our reinsurance division experienced many of the same competitive factors in 1972. A multitude of new organizations entered what has historically been a rather small field, and rates were often cut substantially, and we believe unsoundly, particularly in the catastrophe area. The past year turned out to be unusually free of catastrophes and our underwriting experience was good.

再保险同样被竞争侵蚀：新机构涌入、费率乱砍（尤其巨灾险）。巴菲特的长远计划是成为再保险的重要玩家，但"在费率恶化的背景下立即扩张规模不明智"——等损失变成现实、费率重新定价，才是扩张时机。

George Young has built a substantial and profitable reinsurance operation in just a few years. In the longer term we plan to be a very major factor in the reinsurance field, but an immediate expansion of volume is not sensible against a background of deteriorating rates. In our view, underwriting exposures are greater than ever. When the loss potential inherent in such exposures becomes an actuality, repricing will take place which should give us a chance to expand significantly.

再保险的耐心：“承保风险敞口前所未有地大，等损失变成现实、费率重新定价，就是我们扩张的时机”。George Young 几年内就建起可观且盈利的再保险业务，但巴菲特明确拒绝在费率恶化期冲量。1972 年无重大巨灾是运气，他赌的是必然到来的损失周期——这种“为危机备好弹药”的思维，与 1998 年通用再保险收购形成呼应：先建能力，再等机会。

In the “home state” operation, our oldest and largest such company, Cornhusker Casualty

本州模式的验证。Cornhusker（内布拉斯加）第二年就达到"老牌巨头几十年才有的保费量的三分之一"——本州模式的营销魅力得到证实。明尼苏达和德州的两家小公司亏损，但巴菲特说："承保盈利是成功的标尺，只有承保做对了才能扩张。"

Company, operating in Nebraska only, achieved good underwriting results. In the second full year, the home state marketing appeal has been proven with the attainment of volume on the order of one‐third of that achieved by “old line” giants who have operated in the state for many

decades.

Our two smaller companies, in Minnesota and Texas, had unsatisfactory loss ratios on very small volume. The home state managements understand that underwriting profitably is the yardstick of success and that operations can only be expanded significantly when it is clear that we are doing the right job in the underwriting area. Expense ratios at the new companies are also high, but that is to be expected when they are in the development stage.

扩张的前提：“承保盈利是成功的标尺”。明尼苏达和德州两家新公司量小且赔付率高，但巴菲特把高费用率视为发展期的正常成本，只要承保纪律在就不砍扩张。对比后来很多保险公司为冲规模而放松承保（1970 年代末行业性灾难），这里体现了“先做对，再做大”的顺序。本州模式的核心不是营销，而是以盈利为唯一考核。

John Ringwalt has done an excellent job of launching this operation, and plans to expand into at least one additional state during 1973. While there is much work yet to be done, the home state operation appears to have major long‐range potential.

本州模式开始复制：1973 年至少再进一个州。John Ringwalt 从零建起 Cornhusker 并验证了模式，下一步是异地扩张。注意巴菲特说的是“至少一个”——扩张节奏由承保质量决定，而非管理层 KPI。这一模式后来发展为国民赔偿的“home state”系列，成为 Berkshire 保险版图里现金流最稳的一块。

Last year it was reported that we had acquired Home and Automobile Insurance Company of Chicago. We felt good about the acquisition at the time, and we feel even better now. Led by Vic Raab, this company continued its excellent record in 1972. During 1973 we expect to enter the Florida (Dade County) and California (Los Angeles) markets with the same sort of specialized urban auto coverage which Home and Auto has practiced so successfully in Cook County. Vic has the managerial capacity to run a much larger operation. Our expectation is that Home and Auto will expand significantly within a few years.

Home & Auto 1972 年继续优秀，1973 年计划进入佛罗里达（Dade County）和加州（洛杉矶）市场——用同样的城市专业车险模式复制 Cook County 的成功。Vic Raab 有能力管理更大的业务。

Insurance Investment Results

We were most fortunate to experience dramatic gains in premium volume from 1969 to 1971 coincidental with virtually record‐high interest rates. Large amounts of investable funds were thus received at a time when they could be put to highly advantageous use. Most of these funds were placed in tax‐exempt bonds and our investment income, which has increased from $2,025,201 in 1969 to $6,755,242 in 1972, is subject to a low effective tax rate.

保险浮存金的黄金窗口。1969-71 年保费量大增恰逢利率创历史新高——大量可投资资金以极高回报配置（大部分投进免税债券）。投资收益从 1969 年 $202 万涨到 1972 年 $676 万，而且税负极低。这是浮存金模式的第一次大丰收。

Our bond portfolio possesses unusually good call protection, and we will benefit for many years to come from the high average yield of the present portfolio. The lack of current premium growth, however, will moderate substantially the growth in investment income during the next several years.

债券组合的“反赎回保护”：高息锁死多年。1969-71 年高位收进来的保费投进高息免税债，且几乎无提前赎回风险——意味着这些高收益能持续多年。但巴菲特也提示：保费增长停滞会让未来几年投资收益增速放缓。这里能看到他后来的“浮存金成本”框架雏形：先有低成本的浮存金，才有高收益的投资。

Banking Operations

Our banking subsidiary, The Illinois Bank and Trust Co. of Rockford, maintained its position of industry leadership in profitability. After‐tax earnings of 2.2% on average deposits in 1972 are the more remarkable when evaluated against such moderating factors as: (1) a mix of 50% time deposits heavily weighted toward consumer savings instruments, all paying the maximum rates permitted by law; (2) an unvaryingly strong liquid position and avoidance of money‐market borrowings; (3) a loan policy which has produced a net charge‐off ratio in the last two years of about 5% of that of the average commercial bank. This record is a direct tribute to the leadership of Gene Abegg and Bob Kline who run a bank where the owners and the depositors can both eat well and sleep well.

伊利诺伊银行的极致表现。存款税后收益 2.2%，同时：50% 定期存款（全付法定最高利率）、流动性极强、贷款核销率只有行业平均的 5%。"老板和储户都能吃得好睡得香。"Gene Abegg 和 Bob Kline 是巴菲特心目中银行家的标杆。

During 1972, interest paid to depositors was double the amount paid in 1969. We have aggressively sought consumer time deposits, but have not pushed for large “money market” certificates of deposit although, during the past several years, they have generally been a less costly source of time funds.

存款策略：主动抢居民定期存款，却不去碰更便宜的货币市场大额存单。1972 年付给存户的利息是 1969 年的两倍——银行用让利换稳定负债。巴菲特宁可付出更高成本获取粘性强的存款，也不要来去匆匆的“热钱”，因为负债端的稳定性决定了资产端能做什么。这种“负债质量优先于负债成本”的思路，与后来对保险浮存金质量的强调一脉相承。

During the past year, loans to our customers expanded approximately 38%. This is considerably more than indicated by the enclosed balance sheet which includes $10.9 million in short‐term commercial paper in the 1971 loan total, but which has no such paper included at the end of 1972. Our position as “Rockford’s Leading Bank” was enhanced during 1972. Present rate structures, a decrease in investable funds due to new Federal Reserve collection procedures, and a probable increase in already substantial non‐federal taxes make it unlikely that Illinois

National will be able to increase its earnings during 1973.

贷款增长 38% 的真相：“Rockford 领先银行”地位巩固，但 1973 年盈利难增。资产负债表上贷款增幅被 $1090 万短期商业票据干扰（1971 年计入、1972 年清出），实际增长更快。巴菲特主动预告 1973 年三大逆风：利率结构、联邦储备新清算程序减少可投资资金、州税可能加码——盈利预测从不粉饰，这是 Berkshire 年报一贯的诚实风格。

Financial

On March 15, 1973, Berkshire Hathaway borrowed $20 million at 8% from twenty institutional lenders. This loan is due March 1, 1993, with principal repayments beginning March 1, 1979.

20 年期 $2000 万贷款（8%利率）——为保险扩张备弹药。$900 万还银行借款，其余投入保险子公司。"我们预期保险业务会有显著扩张的机会，必须准备好财务资源去抓住。"——巴菲特在保险业周期底部提前布局。

From the proceeds, $9 million was used to repay our bank loan and the balance is being invested in insurance subsidiaries. Periodically, we expect that there will be opportunities to achieve significant expansion in our insurance business and we intend to have the financial resources available to maximize such opportunities.

Our subsidiaries in banking and insurance have major fiduciary responsibilities to their customers. In these operations we maintain capital strength far above industry norms, but still achieve a good level of profitability on such capital. We will continue to adhere to the former objective and make every effort to continue to maintain the latter.

受托责任：“资本实力远高于行业常态，同时仍在这部分资本上取得良好盈利”。银行和保险拿着客户的钱，Berkshire 宁可让资本冗余、放弃杠杆，也要先保证偿付能力。巴菲特后来多次说：保险业的正确经营方式是“在财务上保守到近乎愚蠢”（1997 年致股东信）。这里的“前者”（资本强度）是底线，“后者”（盈利能力）是追求——顺序从不颠倒。

Warren E. Buffett Chairman of the Board March 16, 1973

1976 Letter

To the Stockholders of Berkshire Hathaway Inc.:

After two dismal years, operating results in 1976 improved significantly. Last year we said the degree of progress in insurance underwriting would determine whether our gain in earnings would be “moderate” or “major.” As it turned out, earnings exceeded even the high end of our expectations. In large part, this was due to the outstanding efforts of Phil Liesche’s managerial group at National Indemnity Company.

V 型反弹：ROE 从 7.6% 跳到 17.3%，利润翻倍。1976 年是 1973-75 年低谷后的强劲反弹。保险行业综合比率从 108.3（亏损）改善到 103.0（仍亏但大幅好转）。National Indemnity 在 Phil Liesche 带领下不仅没有亏损，还实现了高利润——因为 1974-75 年没有跟风降价，现在费率回升时直接受益。

这验证了"反周期策略"的威力：在行业繁荣时（1971-72）主动收缩 → 在行业萧条时（1974-75）不亏钱 → 在行业复苏时（1976）盈利暴增。三步循环，每个周期都在扩大市场份额。巴菲特后来在 2010 年信中说："保险是最适合反周期操作的生意——因为产品是同质的，你不需要在繁荣时抢市场，只需要在萧条时活着。"

In dollar terms, operating earnings came to $16,073,000, or $16.47 per share. While this is a record figure, we consider return on shareholders’ equity to be a much more significant yardstick of economic performance. Here our result was 17.3%, moderately above our long‐ term average and even further above the average of American industry, but well below our record level of 19.8% achieved in 1972.

利润创纪录，但巴菲特只看 ROE：17.3%。每股盈利 $16.47 是历史新高，但他立刻把话题从"赚了多少"转到"资本回报率"——17.3% 高于长期均值、高于美国工业平均，却仍低于 1972 年的 19.8% 纪录。用 ROE 而非 EPS 衡量绩效，是他与当时华尔街的显著分歧：EPS 可以被收购、回购和会计手段操纵，ROE 更接近留存资本的真实生产力。

当时市场的叙事是每股盈利的增长故事，巴菲特却反复强调"一美元留存资本能否创造超过一美元的市值"。他还主动预告 1977 年 ROE 可能回落——先说坏消息、不粉饰数字，是伯克希尔年报的长期传统。这一"ROE 基准"思路在 1977 年信中被正式体系化。

Our present estimate, subject to all the caveats implicit in forecasting, is that dollar operating earnings are likely to improve somewhat in 1977, but that return on equity may decline a bit from the 1976 figure.

Textile Operations

Our textile division was a significant disappointment during 1976. Earnings, measured either by return on sales or by return on capital employed, were inadequate. In part, this was due to industry conditions which did not measure up to expectations of a year ago. But equally important were our own shortcomings. Marketing efforts and mill capabilities were not properly matched in our new Waumbec operation. Unfavorable manufacturing cost variances were produced by improper evaluation of machinery and personnel capabilities. Ken Chace, as always, has been candid in reporting problems and has worked diligently to correct them. He is a pleasure to work with—even under difficult operating conditions.

纺织业再次令人失望——但这次他先检讨自己。1976 年纺织板块的回报"无论按销售还是按资本计都不合格"。一半怪行业（年初预期落空），一半怪自己：新收购的 Waumbec 厂营销与产能错配、对机器和人员能力评估失误。注意他点名的顺序——先行业、后自己，且对 Waumbec 的批评具体到"错误评估"，与 1975 年信里对该收购的乐观形成鲜明对照。

值得学习的是他对 Ken Chace 的态度："即使在困难条件下，和他共事也是乐事。"在巴菲特的价值排序里，经理人的坦诚和勤奋比当期业绩更重要——他深知纺织是日薄西山的生意，问题不在人，而在生意本身。这为 1985 年最终关闭纺织业务埋下了伏笔。

While the first quarter outlook is for red ink, our quite tentative belief is that textile earnings in 1977 will equal, or exceed modestly, those of 1976. Despite disappointing current results, we continue to look for ways to build our textile operation and presently have one moderate‐size acquisition under consideration. It should be recognized that the textile business does not offer the expectation of high returns on investment. Nevertheless, we maintain a commitment to this division—a very important source of employment in New Bedford and Manchester—and believe reasonable returns on average are possible.

明知回报低，为什么还要继续投入？巴菲特罕见地直说："纺织业不提供高投资回报的预期"，却同时宣布维持承诺——因为它是 New Bedford 和 Manchester 重要的就业来源。这是"股东利益 vs 社会责任"的张力第一次被摆在台面上：作为控股股东，他不能像股市投资者那样一走了之。

他还在考虑一笔"中等规模的收购"——在低回报行业继续扩张，符合他"把现有生意经营好"的思路，但也注定这些资本无法流向更高效率的用途。1985 年关闭纺织厂时，他承认这是"以合理价格买入平庸生意"的教训。对普通投资者而言更值得记住的是：承认行业不行，与立即止损，是两回事。

Insurance Underwriting

Casualty insurers enjoyed some rebound from the disaster levels of 1975 as rate increases finally outstripped relentless cost increases. Preliminary figures indicate that the stockholder owned portion of the property and casualty industry had a combined ratio of 103.0 in 1976, compared to 108.3 in 1975. (100 represents a break‐even position on underwriting—and higher figures represent underwriting losses.) We are unusually concentrated in auto lines where stock companies had an improvement from 113.5 to 107.4. Our own overall improvement was even more dramatic, from 115.4 to 98.7.

行业还在亏损，Berkshire 已经转正：综合成本率 98.7 vs 行业 103.0。1976 年财险业总算从 1975 年的灾难中缓过来——费率上调终于跑赢成本上涨。行业综合成本率从 108.3 改善到 103.0（100 为承保盈亏平衡点），整体仍处于亏损；而 Berkshire 从 115.4 跳到 98.7，跨过了 100 这道门槛。

更难得的是 Berkshire 的敞口集中在汽车险——行业里最惨的险种（股票公司 113.5→107.4，依旧亏损）——却实现了整体承保盈利。秘诀仍是反周期：1974-75 年别人降价抢单时 National Indemnity 收缩拒做亏本生意，1976 年费率回升时它成为少数"有能力且愿意"承接业务的承保人，量价齐升。综合成本率从此贯穿巴菲特四十年的年报，是他评估保险生意的核心标尺。

Our major insurance sector in insurance, the traditional auto and general liability business of National Indemnity Company, had an outstanding year, achieving profit levels significantly better than the industry generally. Credit for this performance must be given to Phil Liesche, aided particularly by Roland Miller in Underwriting and Bill Lyons in Claims.

Volume at National Indemnity Company grew rapidly during 1976 as competitors finally reacted to the inadequacy of past rates. But, as mentioned in last year’s annual report, we are concentrated heavily in lines that are particularly susceptible to both economic and social inflation. Thus present rates, which are adequate for today, will not be adequate tomorrow.

"今天的费率够用，明天就不够用了"——通胀是保险的慢性病。1976 年 National Indemnity 保费快速增长，因为竞争对手终于反应过来上调费率。但巴菲特立刻泼冷水：公司集中在最容易受"经济通胀"与"社会通胀"双重冲击的险种——修理成本跑赢整体通胀，责任认定不断扩张，诉讼与天价陪审团判决频发（1975 年信详述过这两股力量）。

这句话是保险周期思维的核心：费率永远滞后于损失成本，"费率合理"只是暂时现象。在通胀年代，今天的承保盈利是明天亏损的种子。所以他拒绝用 1976 年的好结果线性外推长期乐观——这正是他与"趋势外推型"投资者的根本区别。

Our opinion is that before long, perhaps in 1978, the industry will fall behind on rates as temporary prosperity produces unwise competition. If this happens, we must be prepared to meet the next wave of inadequate pricing by a significant reduction in volume.

精准的周期预言："也许在 1978 年，行业费率又将落后。"巴菲特给出明确的行业周期判断：暂时的繁荣会招来不明智的竞争（降价抢单），费率很快会再次跟不上成本；届时 Berkshire 的应对是"大幅收缩业务量"。

注意他的对策不是抱怨或硬扛，而是预先宣布退出纪律：费率不公平时，宁可少做，也不亏本做。这与 1970 年收缩 National Indemnity 的做法一脉相承，也是他后来反复讲的"在别人贪婪时恐惧"。事后看，1978 年行业承保确实再度恶化——反周期者赢在纪律，而不在于预测本身是否精确。

Reinsurance underwriting has lagged the improvement in direct business. When mistakes are made in the pricing of reinsurance, the effects continue for even longer than when similar mistakes are made in direct underwriting. George Young, an outstanding manager, has worked tirelessly to achieve his goal of profitable underwriting, and has cancelled a great many contracts where appropriate rate adjustments were not obtainable. Here, as in the direct business, we have had a concentration in casualty lines which have been particularly hard hit by inflationary conditions. The near term outlook still is not good for our reinsurance business.

再保险的错误"病得更久"，George Young 的办法是直接砍合同。再保险承保改善滞后于直接业务，因为再保险合同的结算链条更长、错误定价的影响期更久。George Young 的应对毫不含糊：拿不到合理费率的合同，大量取消。

这是巴菲特管理哲学的缩影——他看重的不是经理人"把坏生意做好"的执念，而是"放弃坏生意"的意愿与能力。在长尾险种上，一笔错误定价的再保险合同可能拖累多年利润，砍掉它是止损而非失败。对近端展望他照实直说"仍然不好"，不做任何粉饰。

Our “home state” operation continues to make substantial progress under the management of John Ringwalt. The combined ratio improved from 108.4 in 1975 to 102.7 in 1976. There still are some excess costs reflected in the combined ratio which result from the small size of several operations. Cornhusker Casualty Company, oldest and largest of the home state companies, was the winner of the Chairman’s Cup in 1976 for achievement of the lowest loss ratio among the home state companies. Cornhusker also achieved the lowest combined ratio in its history at 94.4, marking the fifth time in its six full years of existence that a ratio below 100 has been recorded. Premium growth was 78% at the home state companies in 1976, as market position improved significantly. We presently plan a new home state operation later this year.

本州模式持续跑赢：Cornhusker 综合成本率 94.4，创历史最低。John Ringwalt 领导的本州公司综合成本率从 108.4 改善到 102.7；旗舰 Cornhusker 以 94.4 创下自身纪录——成立六年来第五次低于 100——并拿下"主席杯"（本州公司最低损失率）。1976 年本州公司保费增长 78%，市场地位显著提升。

本州模式的精髓是：只在一个州经营、深耕单一市场、管理层亲自掌控费率与理赔，规模小到可以"本地化定价"。巴菲特把它当作可以无限复制的模板——信中预告年底前再开一家新公司。"小即是美"的生意哲学，在保险业体现得淋漓尽致。

Our Home and Automobile Insurance Company subsidiary, writing primarily automobile business in the Cook County area of Illinois, experienced a strong recovery in 1976. This is directly attributable to John Seward who, in his first full year, has revamped significantly both rating methods and marketing. The auto business has been shifted to a six month direct bill policy, which permits a faster reaction time to underwriting trends. Our general liability business at Home and Automobile has been expanded significantly with good results. While it remains to be proven that we can achieve sustained underwriting profitability at Home and Auto, we are delighted with the progress John Seward has achieved.

John Seward 的改革：六个月直账单，让费率跟得上市场。Home & Auto 1976 年强劲复苏，直接归功于 Seward 上任第一年对费率方法和营销的双重改革。关键动作是把车险改成"六个月直接账单"（six month direct bill）——每半年直接向客户寄账单、按新费率续保，而不是通过代理人按年续约，从而能更快响应承保趋势的变化。

这是"快"的胜利：通胀环境下损失成本变动快，费率调整周期越短，承保亏损的窟窿越小。巴菲特特意保留谨慎："能否实现持续的承保盈利还有待证明"——他表扬进步，但不提前庆祝。

Overall, we expect a good year in insurance in 1977. Volume is high and present rate levels should allow profitable underwriting. Longer term, however, there are significant negatives in the insurance picture. Auto lines, in particular, seem highly vulnerable to pricing and regulatory problems produced by political and social factors beyond the control of individual companies.

长期警钟：车险是政治与社会因素的俘虏。1977 年保险预计是好年——业务量大、现有费率足以承保盈利。但巴菲特看得更远：车险尤其容易受到定价与监管问题的冲击，而这些问题来自"单个公司无法控制的政治和社会因素"——费率监管、无过错立法、陪审团判决通胀等。

这解释了伯克希尔后来对车险敞口的反复调整，也解释了巴菲特为何偏爱"定价自主权大"的险种。保险业的利润从来不只是精算问题，更是政治问题——这是他在 1970 年代就看清楚、许多同行至今未看清的事实。

Insurance Investments

Pre‐tax investment income in 1976 improved to $10,820,000 from $8,918,000 as invested assets built up substantially, both from better levels of profitability and from gains in premium

volume.

In recent reports we have noted the unrealized depreciation in our bond account, but stated that we considered such market fluctuations of minor importance as our liquidity and general financial strength made it improbable that bonds would have to be sold at times other than those of our choice. The bond market rallied substantially in 1976, giving us moderate net unrealized gains at yearend in the bond portfolios of both our bank and insurance companies.

浮存金哲学的早期表述：债券要能持有到期，市场波动就不重要。1976 年债券市场大涨，银行和保险公司债券组合从浮亏转为小幅浮盈——但巴菲特再次说"无关紧要"，因为他的意图是持有大部分债券到期：只要流动性充足、财务实力允许他只在"自己选的时间"卖出债券，市价波动就只是纸面游戏。

这段话是浮存金（float）投资哲学的雏形：保险先收保费、后付赔款，赔付前沉淀的资金投向债券，只要久期匹配、偿付无忧，就无需在意中途的市值。1977 年信他将系统阐述浮存金概念，此后几十年里，"用别人的钱、按自己的节奏投资"成为伯克希尔的引擎。对债券投资者而言，这是"持有到期"与"盯市估值"之争的早期启蒙。

This, too, is of minor importance since our intention is to hold a large portion of our bonds to maturity. The corollary to higher bond prices is that lower earnings are produced by the new funds generated for investment. On balance, we prefer a situation where our bond portfolio has a current market value less than carrying value, but more attractive rates are available on issues purchased with newly‐generated funds.

Last year we stated that we expected 1976 to be a year of realized capital gains and, indeed, gains of $9,962,000 before tax, primarily from stocks, were realized during the year. It presently appears that 1977 also will be a year of net realized capital gains. We now have a substantial unrealized gain in our stock portfolio as compared to a substantial unrealized loss several years ago. Here again we consider such market fluctuations from year to year relatively unimportant;

unrealized appreciation in our equity holdings, which amounted to $45.7 million at yearend, has declined by about $5 million as this is written on March 21st.

However, we consider the yearly business progress of the companies in which we own stocks to be very important. And here, we have been delighted by the 1976 business performance achieved by most of our portfolio companies. If the business results continue excellent over a period of years, we are certain eventually to achieve good financial results from our stock holdings, regardless of wide year‐to‐year fluctuations in market values.

"股价波动不重要，企业的经营进展才重要。"这是 1976 年信最接近投资哲学总纲的一段：如果组合里公司的经营业绩连续多年优秀，那么无论市值如何大起大落，最终必然收获好的财务结果。年末股票浮盈 $4,570 万，到 3 月 21 日写信时已回落约 $500 万——他对此轻描淡写。

这句话与 1975 年信的"市场波动只提供买入机会"、以及 1987 年信"我们喜欢市场下跌"一脉相承。当时市场主流仍是择时与题材炒作，巴菲特用最朴素的逻辑宣布：股票是企业的一部分，企业赚钱，股东最终赚钱。所谓"长期主义"，在这一段已经完整成型。

持仓表里的主角是 GEICO——这封信里唯一没有评述的大事。年末持仓成本合计约 $5,840 万，其中 GEICO 一项（1,294,308 股普通股 + 1,986,953 股可转换优先股）成本约 $2,350 万，占 40%，是绝对的第一大持仓；按完全稀释口径约占 GEICO 三分之一的股权。而就在几个月前（1976 年初），GEICO 股价从 1972 年的 $61 跌到 $2，公司濒临破产，市场几乎认定它活不过当年。

巴菲特在信中对 GEICO 只字未评——持仓表之外没有一句解释——但这笔交易后来被证明是他职业生涯最伟大的逆向投资之一：新任 CEO Jack Byrne 力挽狂澜，1976 年第四季度公司恢复盈利，股价在一年内从 $2 附近大幅反弹。浮存金 + 恐慌性抛售 = 千载难逢的买入机会，这一课他从 1951 年首次买入 GEICO 时就开始学习，1976 年终于重仓实践（1980 年信他对此做过完整复盘，1996 年伯克希尔全资收购 GEICO 剩余股份）。

注意表中 Washington Post 的成本 $1,063 万——1973-74 熊市的逆向买入此时已浮盈数倍，与 GEICO 一起构成他"危机买入"组合的两大支柱。表格里还有 Interpublic、Ogilvy & Mather 两家广告公司——巴菲特很早就看中广告业的轻资产高回报特性。

年度核心
